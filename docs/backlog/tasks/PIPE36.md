# PIPE36: The pipeline lane's in-daemon phases have no ceiling and Cancel cannot reach two of them, so one hung redaction, transcription or Apple Intelligence pass holds every later meeting until a restart

Band origin: filed 2026-10-25 from the performance review (eight subsystem finders over HEAD 01e904a, every finding adversarially re-verified). Status and priority live in this task's ToC row in [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md).

## Context

Re-verified against HEAD `01e904a` on 2026-10-25 by code reading. Review findings MAINTHREAD-9 and SUBPROCESS-4 (plausible, medium) and SUBPROCESS-5 (confirmed, medium).

**One lane, and only its subprocess is bounded.** `SinkDispatcher` holds `activeJob` from `startNextJobIfNeeded` until `completeActiveJob` (PIPE11's one serial lane), so nothing behind the active job runs until it completes. Two bounds exist and both cover only the `mp` subprocess: `PipelineLauncher.runMP`'s per-spawn watchdog (`defaultMaxRuntime`, 90 min for run-all, SIGTERM then SIGKILL 30 s later), and `SinkDispatcher.startStallTracking` (no heartbeat for `stallThresholdSec`, 30 s, emits `pipeline_stalled` and marks the row stalled), which only `invokePipeline` arms. A full job has three in-daemon phases covered by neither.

**Phase 1, redaction.** `runDaemonTranscription` first awaits `redactor(job.file)`, which is `MuteRedactor.redactIfNeeded`. Its `runFFmpeg` parks a global-queue thread in `proc.waitUntilExit()` inside `withCheckedContinuation`, with no ceiling, and reads stderr only after exit. The row's live entry (`onActiveProgress(loc.stem, PipelineProgress(stage: SinkDispatcher.transcribeStage, elapsedSec: 0))`) is published only after the redactor returns, so during this phase the row has no live entry, offers no Cancel, and cannot stall. It runs only when a non-empty mute timeline exists (capture-first redaction, or a manual off-the-record span). The per-process ceiling and stderr drain for this ffmpeg belong to [CONC5](./CONC5.md) (amended 2026-10-25); this task is the job-level backstop over all three phases.

**Phase 2, FluidAudio.** `try await transcriptionRunner.transcribe(wavURL: job.file, languageHint: languageHint)` has no deadline. Cancel reaches most of it (UX69: FluidAudio's `ChunkProcessor` checks cancellation per chunk), but three parts are not interruptible: `FluidAudioRunner.ensureAsr`'s first-load `AsrModels.downloadAndLoad` plus `loadModels` (a 461 MB download and a CoreML compile; FluidAudio's `DownloadUtils` bounds each request at 1800 s), the synchronous `diarizer.performCompleteDiarization(samples)` with no checkpoint, and any CoreML or Neural Engine prediction that wedges. A re-transcribe (ASR3) runs this phase over an older recording too, which in the owner's iCloud-synced library may be dataless ([STOR6](./STOR6.md) owns that read). The healthy rate makes a hang easy to tell from a long meeting: 52.5 s for a 110-minute meeting (UX69, `20260716-163031`), and 12.5 s for the 2,078 s `20261016-095827` (events.jsonl, `pipeline_started` at 07:33:45.892Z to `engine_succeeded` at 07:33:58.425Z on 2026-10-16).

**Phase 3, Apple Intelligence.** When run-all leaves `<stem>.apple_pending.json`, `PipelineLauncher.runAll`'s completion calls `completeViaAppleIntelligence`, which starts an unretained `Task { try await AppleIntelligenceSummarizer().summarizeFile(...) }` and then `self.runMP(["publish", summaryJSON.path], timeout: 10 * 60, meeting: summaryJSON)` with no `retain:`, so `.none`. By then run-all's `.run` slot was released in its termination handler and `transcriptionTask` has finished, so `SinkDispatcher.cancelActiveJob` signals nothing: it logs `pipeline_cancelled` and the summarize and publish carry on. `AppleIntelligenceSummarizer.generate` maps `maxWindowChars` 1000 windows at `overlapChars` 200 one after another, with a fresh `LanguageModelSession` per `respondSummary`, then reduces in rounds of `reduceBatch` 4, with no `Task.checkCancellation` and no ceiling. Measured density: 22,796 transcript characters for the 2,078 s `20261016-095827` (`run_skipped` `apple_pending` in pipeline_events.jsonl, 2026-10-16), about 11 a second, so a 3 h meeting is about 118k characters, about 148 map calls and 51 reduce calls, about 200 sequential on-device calls. Each call is bounded by the model's 4096-token context, so a hold that never ends needs a framework hang; the common cost is a long hold with a Cancel that does nothing. And because this phase emits no heartbeat while the stall timer `invokePipeline` armed is still running, any Apple summarize longer than 30 s flips the row to stalled while it is working normally. Never observed on the owner's Mac: the three Apple runs on record (2026-10-16) were rejected on the first window as an unsupported language within 0.1 s ([PIPE25](./PIPE25.md)), and `config.toml`'s backend is `anthropic` on 2026-10-25.

**What the comments and the guide promise.** `SinkDispatcher.cancelActiveJob`'s doc says a full job has two halves and both are signalled, `runDaemonTranscription` says "a Cancel that cannot stop the work is worse than no Cancel at all", `PipelineLauncher.defaultMaxRuntime` says it ensures the daemon never sits in `.handoff` indefinitely, and [GUIDE.md](../../GUIDE.md) says the row carries a Cancel "the whole time" and that stopping is safe at any point. All of these hold for the subprocess and the parts UX69 covered; none holds for the Apple phase, which UX69 never touched, so this is a gap rather than a UX69 regression.

**Cost when it fires.** The row reads Transcribing (or has no entry, during redaction) indefinitely, every later meeting queues behind it with no notification, and only a daemon restart clears the lane, which then marks the stem interrupted. The installed daemon measured on 2026-10-25 had been up 7 days 8 hours, so a hang early in such a window is days of unprocessed meetings. Each trigger is rare; the review rated the class medium.

## Scope

1. **A job deadline over the transcription half.** In `SinkDispatcher.startNextJobIfNeeded`, when `transcriptionTask` starts, arm a GCD timer on the main queue (a `DispatchSourceTimer`, not `Task.sleep` or `runWithTimeout`, per `daemon/CLAUDE.md`'s CI8 rule: a synchronous diarizer can hold the cooperative pool such a timer would need). Its length comes from a pure `SinkDispatcher.transcriptionDeadline(audioSeconds:runnerWarm:)`: an audio-scaled inference allowance plus a first-load allowance only while the runner has not completed a job, both constants set at no less than 20 times the rates measured above, with the reasoning beside them. The duration comes from the existing header read, `RecordingPostProcessor.audioDurationSec(of:)`, falling back to a generous fixed deadline when it returns nil. The timer is cancelled when the job hands off to `invokePipeline` or `invokeFinalize`, or completes.
2. **On expiry, fail the job and move on.** Mark the job abandoned, cancel `transcriptionTask`, write `<stem>.error.json` at stage `.transcribe` for `.full` jobs (as today's failure path does) with a reason that names the timeout and the way back, emit `transcription_timed_out` (category `transcription`) with `file`, `audio_seconds`, `elapsed_sec`, `phase` (`redact` or `transcribe`, from a phase the job updates) and `runner_warm`, and complete the job as a failure so the next one starts. A deadline in the redaction phase runs nothing downstream, so no unredacted audio is transcribed or published; SEC16's quarantine stays the redactor's own `.failed` outcome.
3. **Fence the late return.** `completeActiveJob` ignores a job whose id is not `activeJob`'s, and every exit of `runDaemonTranscription` for an abandoned job writes nothing: no `<stem>.json`, no `invokePipeline`, no sidecar over the timeout reason. Today a late return would clear `activeJob` and start the next queued job beside the one already running, breaking PIPE11's one lane, and its cancellation catch would overwrite the timeout reason with `cancelledReason`.
4. **Do not reuse a suspect runner.** `SinkDispatcher.transcriptionRunner` is a `let`; inject a factory defaulting to `TranscriptionService.makeRunner` and build a fresh runner for the next job after an expiry, so a wedged `AsrManager` is not handed the next meeting. Model memory is briefly doubled while the old call is stuck (461 MB plus 13 MB of models on disk, measured 2026-10-25), which is the accepted cost.
5. **Make the Apple phase cancellable, bounded and honest about progress.** In `PipelineLauncher`: hold the Apple `Task` where `cancelActiveRun` cancels it; call `try Task.checkCancellation()` before each map and reduce call in `AppleIntelligenceSummarizer.generate`, so a cancelled summarize completes as a cancellation (`PipelineLauncher.isCancellation`) and lands as UX50's `.interrupted`; pass `retain: .run` to the follow-up `runMP(["publish", ...])`; bound each `respondSummary` call (it suspends, so `runWithTimeout` is the right tool here) at a ceiling the pickup sets from a measured call on this Mac, failing with a new `AppleIntelligenceError.timedOut` that `isDeterministicBackendFailure` does not include, so PIPE25's fallback never fires on it; and beat `onProgress` once per call (stage `summarize`, `stemsDone` and `stemsTotal` as calls done and planned) through the `onProgress` that `runAll` already receives, so the stall flag means what it says.

## Explicitly not

- Raising `maxWindowChars` to cut the call count. Its own comment records an overflow at 3,200 characters on Ukrainian (LOCAL3), and [ARCH11](./ARCH11.md)'s provider leg replaces this loop with the Python chunker. If that leg lands first, item 5 shrinks to a ceiling on its `fm-complete` child.
- The redaction ffmpeg's own ceiling, kill and stderr drain: [CONC5](./CONC5.md), amended 2026-10-25. With both, the per-process ceiling fires first and this deadline is the backstop.
- Offering Cancel during the redaction phase. It is short on a healthy file (the merge ffmpeg ran at about 1,100 times real time on 2026-10-16), and a Cancel there needs the redaction child to be killable first, which is CONC5's runner.
- Killing a stuck CoreML call. There is no cooperative way to; the job is abandoned and the runner replaced.
- Dataless iCloud reads during a re-transcribe: [STOR6](./STOR6.md).
- The unsupported-language fallback in the same function: [PIPE25](./PIPE25.md). Whichever lands second keeps both.
- A ceiling over the `mp` half: `runMP`'s watchdog already bounds it.
- Quitting or restarting with a job in flight, which leaves the `mp` child unsupervised: filed separately from the same review.

## Acceptance

- `SinkDispatcherTests` with an injected redactor that never returns and a short injected deadline: the job completes as a failure with stage `.transcribe` and the timeout reason, `transcription_timed_out` is emitted with `phase` `redact`, nothing downstream ran, and the next queued job starts.
- The same with an injected `TranscriptionRunner` whose `transcribe` ignores cancellation and never returns: the job fails with `phase` `transcribe`, and the next job runs on a freshly built runner (the factory is called twice).
- A late-return test: the stuck runner returns while the next job is active; `activeJob` is still the second job, no third job started, no `<stem>.json` or pipeline spawn happened for the abandoned stem, and its `error.json` still carries the timeout reason.
- A job that finishes inside its deadline emits no timeout event and is otherwise unchanged; the pure `transcriptionDeadline` returns the documented values, with the first-load allowance only for a cold runner.
- `PipelineLauncherTests` with an injected summarizer seam (share PIPE25's if it has landed): Cancel during the map loop stops before the next call, completes as a cancellation and spawns no publish; a call past the per-call ceiling fails with `.timedOut`; `cancelActiveRun()` after the summarize terminates the publish spawn; `onProgress` fires once per planned call, so a healthy run never trips the stall timer.
- `transcription_timed_out` is in `CONVENTIONS.md`'s event table and `python3 scripts/truth_fences.py events` is green; `docs/GUIDE.md` section 10, "A meeting that failed", names the timeout reason, in the same commit.
- `cd daemon && swift build` and `swift test` green, N new tests in the commit body; `bash scripts/ci-local.sh` green.
