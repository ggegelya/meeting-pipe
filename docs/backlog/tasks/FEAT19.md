# FEAT19: the roster learns only from clicks, the naming sheet is a blank text field with exact-case matching and no merge, and its copy promises recognition the matcher delivers about a third of the time

Band origin: filed 2026-10-24 from the 2026-10-24 audit band ([go-live assessment](../../audits/2026-10-21-go-live-assessment.md) FQ-1, UX-7, FQ-15, and its "Improving transcription and people identification" item 2). Status and priority live in this task's ToC row in [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md).

## Context

FEAT3-ROSTER built the roster, [FEAT14](./FEAT14.md) surfaced the near miss, and [FEAT17](./FEAT17.md) measured it on 2026-08-19: GO, 31 of 83 meetings would have offered a suggestion, but `below_floor` held 80.1 percent of declines and the median top-candidate similarity across 504 labels was 0.481, under `SUGGEST_MIN = 0.50`. FEAT17's own reading: "the feature is worth having and is not the bottleneck; enrolment is." The go-live assessment measured the same library at 167 of 504 voices auto-named (33 percent), and zero for a new user with an empty roster. Verified in code on 2026-10-24, the reasons enrolment stays thin are structural:

1. **No passive enrolment.** The only call to `RosterStore.enroll` in `pipeline/src` is `roster_cmd.py`'s `roster.enroll(args.name, embedding)`, reached only when the owner names a voice. `resolve_speaker_labels` in `pipeline/src/mp/diarize.py` accepts a roster match and writes the name, but never folds the matched embedding back in. The self voiceprint already does exactly this: `orchestrate._me_signals` calls `store.update(speaker_embeddings[mic_me])` on every meeting until `_VOICEPRINT_ENROLL_CAP = 12`. So the owner's voice learns passively and nobody else's does.
2. **The naming sheet is a blank field.** `SpeakerNamingSheet` in `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/TranscriptTab.swift` is a single `TextField("Name", text: $name)`. It offers none of the people already enrolled, so the owner retypes from memory.
3. **Exact-case identity, no merge.** `RosterStore.enroll` finds the person with `p.get("name") == name`, so "Dana" and "dana" become two people with two thin centroids. `RosterStore.rename` refuses a collision on purpose ("renaming is not merging"), and there is no `merge` anywhere: not in `RosterStore`, not in `roster_cmd.py`'s subparsers (`enroll`, `list`, `forget`, `rename`), not in the Preferences People pane.
4. **No way to hear a voice before naming it.** `VoiceResolutionBar.actions(for:)` offers `.name`, `.rename`, `.unname`, `.renameEverywhere` and `.suggest`. Nothing plays the cluster's audio, so naming an "Unknown B" means scrolling to find one of its lines.
5. **Naming one meeting does not reach the others.** Enrolling helps only meetings finalized afterwards. An older meeting picks the new person up only through a full ASR3 Re-transcribe, because `mp finalize` re-matches from the `speaker_embeddings` the daemon wrote into `<stem>.json`, which `finalize_streamed_transcript` pops and strips at first finalize. The per-label vectors survive only in `<stem>.embeddings.json`, keyed by final label, and nothing re-matches from there.
6. **The copy overpromises (FQ-15).** `SpeakerNamingSheet`'s enroll explanation says "so this person is named automatically in future meetings"; `docs/GUIDE.md` says "From then on that voice is recognised automatically in later meetings" and, in the glossary, "A voice you name once is recognised in later meetings". At 33 percent that is a promise the matcher keeps a third of the time.

## Scope

Five legs, each landable on its own, in this order. Leg 1 is the one FEAT17's verdict points at.

1. **Passive enrolment on confident matches.** In `resolve_speaker_labels` (or a small helper beside it in `roster.py`), when the two-gate rule accepts a match by a margin well clear of the gates, fold that cluster's embedding into the person. Constraints, all pinned by tests: a passive bar of its own, above `MATCH_MIN` and above the 0.644 that [DIAR5](./DIAR5.md) measured as the highest similarity between two people the owner's corrections prove different, set from the owner's corpus with FEAT17's read-only probe pattern and documented beside the constant; at most one passive sample per person per meeting, idempotent on re-finalize (keyed by stem, so an ASR3 batch never enrols the same meeting twice); hand-named samples are never evicted by passive ones (today `samples[-_MAX_SAMPLES:]` drops the oldest, so a run of passive samples would push out the owner's own picks and let a centroid drift toward whatever it last matched); `MATCH_MIN`, `MATCH_MARGIN` and `SUGGEST_MIN` do not move. Emit `pipeline.roster_passive_enrolled` with person and stem, and add it to the `CONVENTIONS.md` events table.
2. **A name picker.** `SpeakerNamingSheet` lists enrolled people (the `rosterNames` `VoiceResolution.derive` already receives) as completions under the field. A typed name that matches an enrolled one case-insensitively resolves to the enrolled spelling, and `RosterStore.enroll` applies the same rule as defence in depth.
3. **Merge.** `RosterStore.merge(source, into)` concatenates samples (respecting the leg 1 provenance rule), recomputes centroids and removes `source`; `mp roster merge --from --into`; a **Merge into...** action on the Preferences People pane. Past meetings follow through the overlay carry `RosterRename.carry` already performs for a rename, never by rewriting `<stem>.json`.
4. **Play a sample.** A **Play a sample** chip action that seeks the existing player to the cluster's longest segment and plays it, for every voice that has segments.
5. **Apply to my other meetings.** After a naming, offer to re-match the same person across the library from each meeting's `<stem>.embeddings.json`, with no ASR and no engine call, writing names into the speaker-label overlay (reversible, the FEAT3-UNDO contract) for every `THEM-x` the two-gate rule now accepts. Report the count touched. It uses the same gates as finalize, so it can never name a voice finalize would have refused.

Copy (FQ-15), in the same commit as leg 1: the enroll explanation, GUIDE's "Naming someone so it sticks" recipe and glossary entry, and the People-pane footer in `PreferencesView+Pipeline.swift` say the app recognises a named voice "when it is confident", not always.

## Explicitly not

- Per-span scoring against roster centroids using FluidAudio's per-chunk embeddings, which `FluidAudioRunner.weightedMeanEmbeddings` averages into one vector per cluster. It is a matcher change; measure leg 1's effect first, then decide.
- Lowering any gate. FEAT14's "Decisions worth not simplifying away" settles why; do not reopen it.
- Naming latency. That is [PERF8](./PERF8.md), still owner-gated on its profiler capture; leg 5 touches many overlays and should land after PERF8's self-write suppression if that has shipped.
- The owner's own unlabelled voice and the empty `user_label`, which is [UX83](./UX83.md). Name candidates from meeting apps or the calendar are [FEAT20](./FEAT20.md). Publishing before naming is [FEAT21](./FEAT21.md).
- Voiceprint file modes and a forget-everywhere cascade ([SEC22](./SEC22.md)) and opt-in recognition posture ([SEC24](./SEC24.md), P3). Leg 1 increases how many embeddings are retained, which SEC24 must account for if it is promoted.

## Acceptance

- `pipeline` tests: a confident match enrols one sample; a match between `MATCH_MIN` and the passive bar enrols nothing; re-finalizing the same stem enrols nothing new; twelve passive samples never evict a hand-named one; the three gate constants keep their asserted values; `enroll("dana")` after `enroll("Dana")` grows one person; `merge` folds samples and removes the source; the leg 5 re-match names exactly the labels `match_detail` accepts and writes only the overlay.
- `daemon` tests: the picker resolves a case-variant to the enrolled spelling; `VoiceResolutionBar.actions(for:)` offers Play a sample for a voice with segments; the merge carry rewrites overlays the way the rename carry does.
- `cd pipeline && uv run --extra dev ruff check src tests`, `pyright` and `pytest -q` green; `cd daemon && swift build` and `swift test` green; new test counts in each commit body; `python3 scripts/truth_fences.py both` green with the new event documented.
- `docs/GUIDE.md` recipes for naming, merging and playing a sample updated in the same commit as each control (DOC11).
- Evidence after a week of meetings (confirms, does not gate): re-run FEAT17's probe; median top-candidate similarity and the auto-named share both rise against the 2026-08-19 figures (0.481, and 33 percent from the go-live read).
