# REC30: A capture channel that starts late or runs short is never checked per channel, so 14 to 16 s of one side went missing with no stall, loss or parity event

Band origin: filed 2026-11-13 (audit band 2026-11-13, see [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md)). Status and priority live in this file's header; [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) lists every task.

## Context

Source findings: field evidence "Channel audio went missing in two recordings with no stall, loss or parity event" and "System audio started late or ran short on a tenth of recordings" in [2026-11-13-field-evidence.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-field-evidence.md) (sections "Mic, mute and capture health" and "Daemon health and cross-check of the code audit"); the low "One long meeting is two thirds silence between turns of one side" from the "Transcripts and summaries" section of the same file is folded in below. The pack is the work laptop's logs and meeting files, 2026-09-29 to 2026-11-13, without audio, so every gap below is inferred from logged durations and buffer counts.

**What the field shows.** 74 recordings over three build eras. Baselines: channel gap (mic audio minus head offset plus system audio) median -0.06 s, p90 0.52 s; mic shortfall (span to the recorder's stop minus mic audio) median -0.02 s, p90 0.15 s; head offset median 0.53 s, p90 4.24 s, max 33.21 s. The outliers:

- `20261015-170719`: `system_capture_start_timed_out` at 2026-10-15T14:07:30.032Z, retry first buffer 19.7 s after the loss, head offset 33.21 s (REC17 behaving as written, and announced). Beyond that, the mic holds 2,013.8 s against 2,024.5 s from recorder start to the recorder's stop (10.7 s short) and the system file holds 1,964.72 s (98,236 buffers of 0.02 s), 15.9 s shorter than the mic after its head offset. The system track is 49.08 s shorter than the mic in all.
- `20261015-144207`: system channel 14.4 s shorter than the mic after its 2.03 s head offset, mic not short, no loss event. Its stop began 20.2 s after the stop decision; peers with the same delay show no such gap (0.2, 2.0, -0.6, -2.5 s), and because durations are totals the pack cannot tell a stream that stopped about 14 s early from one that lost that much mid-call.
- Three more with a 2.0 to 3.7 s system gap (`20260930-175206`, `20261015-103042`, `20261022-103101`); `20261021-103054` with a 17.38 s head offset; 8 recordings whose channels differ by more than 5 s.
- None of these windows has `mic_tap_stall_detected`, `system_capture_stall_detected`, a configuration change, a second `system_capture_lost` or `duration_parity_low` (0 in the whole log). Both large cases sit on 2026-10-15, the day with 829 of the 1,656 `shareable_content_unavailable` rows (395 and 281 inside these two windows) and 6 of the 13 torn event lines. Era 3 (from 2026-11-12, 6 recordings) has every gap within 0.2 s, so the class is not seen at the newest build, but nothing at HEAD would report it if it recurred.

**Why nothing fired** (re-verified at HEAD `3a2da4fc` on 2026-11-13):

- `MeetingRecorder.mergeIntermediates` reads both durations and emits `recorder`/`intermediate_durations` (`mic_audio_sec`, `system_audio_sec`, `delta_sec`, `wallclock_sec`, `head_offset_sec`) and then merges. Nothing compares them with each other or with the span; the comment calls it a "diagnostic for the reported end-of-call skew".
- `MeetingRecorder.checkDurationParity` runs from `persistAfterMerge` on the merged final, `checkDurationParity(file: final, recordedFor: Date().timeIntervalSince(started))`. REC9's merge pads both legs to the target, so a channel that is short inside the final is invisible to a whole-file ratio, and `RecordingDurationParity.minimumRatio` is 0.85 anyway. The wallclock is read after the merge, so it includes stop and merge time (merge median 2.9 s, p90 11.0 s in the field). Both large cases read 99%.
- `MeetingSessionController.remoteAudioWarning(systemFires:degraded:)` returns `.micOnly` for zero frames and `.interrupted` only while `systemDegraded` is still set. A recovered outage, a late start and a short tail all return `.none`. REC17 named the padded-head half of this under "observed but not filed here" ("whether the stop should also say the first N minutes of the other side are missing is a separate UX question"); this task files it.

**Correction at filing: the system channel already has a 1 Hz buffer-count watchdog.** The batch asked for the mic's watchdog to be applied to the system channel. `SystemAudioChannel.startSystemLivenessWatchdog` already runs a 1 s main-queue timer over `MicTapLivenessMonitor(stallAfterSeconds: 3.0)` and emits `system_capture_stall_detected` then `handleSystemAudioLost` (REC4, gated per attempt by REC17's `systemFires > systemFiresAtArm`). It did not fire in either window, so whatever lost 14 to 16 s either arrived as buffers spaced under 3 s apart, or as buffers that were delivered and not written, or fell outside a live capture (before the first buffer, or after the halt). That is why this task measures where the audio went rather than adding a second watchdog. A write-side loss under 32 consecutive failures is [REC22](./REC22.md)'s and leaves no trace today either.

**Correction at filing: the folded low is REC17's own incident.** `20260917-143029` (5,545 s, 63% of the time after the first word in long word gaps, one of 2,456 s, "no recorder events exist for that date") is the recording [REC17](./REC17.md) was filed from: `SCStream started` 64 min 12 s after `recorder started`, `head_offset_sec: 3852.29`, `system_audio_sec: 1695.56` against `mic_audio_sec: 5548.10`. The field pack's event log starts 2026-09-29, which is why it saw no events; REC17's spec quotes them from the owner's log. The far side was missing from the start to minute 64, which matches the gaps the transcript shows. No owner listening check is needed, and it is not a separate case to trace; it is the strongest example of what this task's stop-time notice would have said, since REC17's bound now cuts that outage to 10 s plus a retry but still announces nothing at stop once the retry recovers.

**Adjacent, not this task.** The head offset itself may be overstated by 1.3 to 2.6 s in 8 recordings because the system stamp is taken at the first callback: [REC33](./REC33.md). Until that lands, a head offset between 1 and 3 s is partly measurement error, which is why the notice threshold below sits well above it. [REC28](./REC28.md) (done 2026-11-12) touched the adjacent given-up capture path and is not in any installed build; [REC16](./REC16.md) and [REC8](./REC8.md) own the outage padding and the automatic retry.

## Scope

1. **Stamp where each channel's audio lies.** On the delivery paths that already count buffers (the mic tap that bumps `micFires`, and the `SystemAudioCapture` closure in `SystemAudioChannel.startSystemCapture` that bumps `systemFires`), record the `systemUptime` of the first and the latest buffer and the frames delivered, per file open (reset where `systemFirstFrameUptime` is reset today). One aligned store per buffer on the render and delivery threads, no lock, no allocation, matching how `systemFirstFrameUptime` is written.
2. **Take the span at capture stop.** `MeetingRecorder.endSession` snapshots the capture-stop uptime into `StopSnapshot`, beside `systemHeadOffsetSec`. `intermediate_durations` and the new check use it instead of `Date()` at merge time, and `persistAfterMerge` passes the same span to `checkDurationParity` so the parity wallclock no longer includes the merge.
3. **One pure verdict.** A `ChannelCoverage.evaluate` (new, pure, static, beside `RecordingDurationParity`) over the snapshot: mic audio, system audio, head offset, the capture span, and each channel's first and last buffer uptimes and delivered frames. It returns the mic shortfall, the channel gap, the system tail (capture stop minus the system's last buffer), and delivered-versus-written seconds per channel, so a short file reads as one of four causes: started late, stopped early, gaps mid-call (delivered seconds below the live span), or delivered but not written.
4. **Emit and surface.** When the mic shortfall, the channel gap or the head offset exceeds 2 s, emit `recorder`/`channel_coverage_short` once per recording with the measured fields and the cause. When the far side is missing for 10 s or more in total (head offset plus channel gap, or the tail), extend `remoteAudioWarning` with a case that names the missing seconds and route it to the existing remote-audio notice copy, so the owner learns at stop that the other side is missing for part of the meeting. 2 s is above every era-3 recording and the field baseline p90; 10 s separates the 14 to 33 s cases from the 2.0 to 3.7 s ones and from REC33's suspected 1.3 to 2.6 s overstatement.

## Explicitly not

- A second system watchdog: one exists (see the correction above). Changing its 3 s stall threshold is not in scope.
- Root-causing the 2026-10-15 losses. The instrumentation in items 1 and 3 is what makes the next one placeable; `shareable_content_unavailable` on that day is a correlation, not a cause.
- A persistent Library mark (a `<stem>.meta.json` key read by the pipeline or the Library). It is a cross-tree sidecar change (`CONVENTIONS.md#sidecar-schema-stem-metajson`, both writers and readers, `test_workflow_overlay.py`) and the stop notice plus the event cover the ask; file it separately if the owner wants the mark to outlive the notification.
- Correcting the head offset stamp: [REC33](./REC33.md). Mic re-arm under-padding: [REC16](./REC16.md). Write failures under 32 in a row: [REC22](./REC22.md). The no-ffmpeg mic-only fallback: [DEP2](./DEP2.md).
- Retrying a discarded late SCStream sooner (the field's other recommendation on `20261015-170719`, where the 19.7 s wait roughly doubled the loss): REC17 and REC8 territory, not filed here.

## Acceptance

- `ChannelCoverageTests` (pure): a system channel that starts 15 s late returns started-late with a 15 s head offset; one that stops 15 s before capture stop returns stopped-early with a 15 s tail; one whose delivered seconds fall 15 s below its live span returns gaps mid-call; one whose delivered seconds exceed the written file returns not-written; a mic 10.7 s short of the span (the `20261015-170719` numbers) is reported; and the field baselines (gap -0.06 s, shortfall -0.02 s, offset 0.53 s) and a 2.5 s REC33-shaped over-delay return nothing.
- Recorder tests with a fake system channel that starts 15 s late and one that stops 15 s early: each emits `channel_coverage_short` once with the cause, and `remoteAudioWarning` returns the new case so the stop notice fires; a 3 s gap emits the event and no notice.
- A test that `checkDurationParity` receives the capture-stop span, not a post-merge `Date()`, and a source fence that `persistAfterMerge` no longer reads `Date()` for it.
- `channel_coverage_short` documented in `CONVENTIONS.md`'s event-log section the way REC20's `recorder` actions are; `python3 scripts/truth_fences.py events` green. `docs/GUIDE.md` names the new stop notice where it covers the remote-audio notices, in the same commit.
- `cd daemon && swift build` and `swift test` green, with the number of new tests in the commit body; `bash scripts/ci-local.sh` green.
