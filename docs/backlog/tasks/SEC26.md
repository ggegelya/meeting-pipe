# SEC26: a recording held back by SEC16, or a quarantined orphan, is moved as its only copy into `originals/`, which `OriginalsReaper` permanently deletes after 30 days or past 10 GB, while the row says the recording "is kept" and offers a Retry that cannot work

Band origin: filed 2026-10-25 from the 2026-10-25 observability audit band ([observability audit](../../audits/2026-10-25-observability-audit.md) row O14, finders UI-4, SW-13, SW-14). Status and priority live in this task's ToC row in [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md).

## Context

`originals/` (`MuteRedactor.originalsDirectory()`, `~/Library/Application Support/MeetingPipe/originals`) was built for one kind of file: the pre-redaction full recording whose redacted twin stays in `raw/`. SEC16 and the orphan sweep later started using it for a second kind, a recording that must not be consumed and has no twin. The reaper never learned the difference. Re-read in code on 2026-10-25.

**Held back means moved, not copied.** `SinkDispatcher`'s job path: `if case .failed(let reason) = await redactor(job.file) { let kept = quarantineHeldBack(job.file) ... }`. `quarantineHeldBack` calls `MuteRedactor.quarantine(wav, in: originalsDir)`, and `SinkDispatcher` is built without an `originalsDir`, so it lands in the shared folder; `quarantine` is `try FileManager.default.moveItem(at: url, to: dest)`. `OrphanRecordingRecovery.quarantine` uses the same function for a redaction-owed orphan whose timeline was lost. originals/ is excluded from Time Machine (`protectOriginalAtRest`) and from `mp backup` (ADR 0016), so no other copy exists.

**The reaper deletes it.** `OriginalsReaper.sweep` lists the folder's top level and `decideReap` filters on `contentModificationDate` and size alone: `maxAge = 30 * 24 * 60 * 60`, then oldest-first past `maxTotalBytes` (10 GB). Deletion is `removeItem`, not the Trash. No marker, name or sidecar is consulted. It runs from `Coordinator.reapStorage` at launch and after every job. The `originals_reaped` event records only `count` and `bytes_freed`, not which files.

**Every surface says it is kept.** `SinkDispatcher.heldBackReason`: "The full recording is kept in MeetingPipe's originals folder." The orphan notification in `Coordinator`: "They are in the MeetingPipe originals folder." docs/GUIDE.md: "the full recording is moved into the private recovery folder". README.md describes the 30-day reap only for recovery copies whose redacted twin stays in raw/ (GUIDE.md's "reclaimed after a month or so" sentence is about those too).

**Retry is offered and fails.** `MeetingDetailView`'s failure notice passes a non-nil `retry` closure gated only on in-flight processing, `FailureNotice` renders the button whenever `retry` is non-nil, and `MeetingRow.inlineFix` returns `.retryRun` for every `.failed` row. `heldBackReason`'s doc comment says its prefix is one `FailurePresentation.isValidation` knows "so the row explains itself instead of offering a retry", but `isValidation` only chooses the explanation text; it suppresses no button. `MeetingLibraryService.retryMeeting` skips the transcript-reuse branch because the stage is `.transcribe`, finds no final in raw/, and returns `LibraryError.noAudio` ("No audio for `<stem>` - cannot retry").

**Two redactor logs say something that did not happen (SW-13).** `MuteRedactor.partitionSpans` treats a span whose level cannot be read as withheld, and `micChannelMeanDb` returns nil when `try? AVAudioFile(forReading: wav)` fails. So when the final cannot be opened at all, every automatic mute span is withheld and logged as `"reason": "speech_in_muted_span"`; with no manual span, the redactor deletes the timeline and returns `.notNeeded`, and the full audio goes on to a transcription that will fail on the same unreadable file. Separately, a failed promote runs `try? FileManager.default.moveItem(at: originalsURL(for: wav, in: dir), to: wav)` and then logs "restored full recording" unconditionally; if that move fails, the only copy sits in the reaped top level of originals/ while `quarantineHeldBack` finds no WAV to hold and reports `kept: null`.

**At-rest protection is `try?` (SW-14).** `protectOriginalAtRest` sets 0600 and `isExcludedFromBackup` with `try?` on both, though its doc comment says both write sites funnel through it "so neither can silently drift". `TranscriptionRunner` sets the transcript's 0600 the same way after an `.atomic` write that created the file at the umask default.

Not executed on this Mac: no `mute_redaction_failed`, `recording_held_back` or `orphan_quarantined` events exist in events.jsonl, and there is no originals/ folder. Reachable whenever SEC16's fail-closed path fires (ffmpeg missing, a failed run, a lost or unreadable timeline on a meeting that owed a cut); the reaper half runs after every job.

## Scope

1. **A separate folder the reaper never touches.** `MuteRedactor.heldDirectory()` is `originals/held/`. `MuteRedactor.quarantine` moves into it (both callers), with the same `protectOriginalAtRest`. `OriginalsReaper.sweep` considers regular files only (`isRegularFileKey`), so the subfolder is never a candidate, and `StorageStatsStore`'s originals figure includes `held/` and reports it as its own line. The meeting delete cascade in `MeetingLibraryService` (the `originalsDir().appendingPathComponent("\(stem).wav")` removal) also removes `held/<stem>.wav`. Held files are deleted only by the owner.
2. **Reveal, not Retry, on a held row.** `MeetingLibraryService` exposes `heldRecordingURL(stem:)` (the file exists in `held/`). When it is non-nil, the detail failure notice and the row's inline fix offer **Show recording** (`NSWorkspace.activateFileViewerSelecting`) instead of Retry, and `retryMeeting` returns a validation-prefixed error rather than `noAudio` if reached anyway. `heldBackReason` drops nothing but gains "It is not deleted automatically."
3. **A held-back promote failure keeps its copy.** The redactor checks the restore move. On failure it logs `ERROR: could not restore full recording for <file>; it remains in originals` and adds `restored: false` to the `mute_redaction_failed` event; `quarantineHeldBack`, finding no WAV in raw/ but a copy at `originalsURL(for:)`, moves that copy into `held/`.
4. **An unreadable final is its own failure.** `redact` opens the final once before partitioning; if `AVAudioFile(forReading:)` throws it returns `fail(.audioUnreadable, ...)` (new `FailureReason`, `audio_unreadable`), which holds the meeting back through the existing SEC16 path. Per-span unmeasurable ranges (a span past the file's end) stay withheld but are counted apart: `mute_redaction_withheld` carries `speech_spans` and `unmeasured_spans` instead of one guessed reason.
5. **Protection results are checked.** `protectOriginalAtRest` returns which protection failed, and its callers plus `TranscriptionRunner`'s chmod log a `recorder.log` WARN and `Log.event(category: "recorder", action: "at_rest_protection_failed", ...)` with the file, the protection, and the error's domain and code. The move still happens: refusing it would leave the full recording in raw/, which is the iCloud-synced tree on the owner's Mac (STOR6) and strictly worse.

## Explicitly not

- An in-app **Restore** that moves a held recording back and processes it uncut. That publishes the audio SEC16 refused to publish, so it is an owner decision with its own task, as the SEC16 gotcha in daemon/docs/gotchas.md already says of a recovery UI.
- Giving a quarantined orphan a Library row. It has no `<stem>.error.json` and no audio in raw/ (recovery may have restored its `<stem>.meta.json` before the move); the one-shot notification gains the same "not deleted automatically" sentence and the Storage line from item 1 is its durable surface. Wrong failure titles and Details lost after relaunch: UX93.
- A retention policy or size cap for `held/`. Recorded so it is not forgotten if held files ever accumulate; today they never have.
- The crash-recovery markers that decide whether an orphan is quarantined at all: REC21. Other best-effort chmod sweeps (`Logger`, `MeetingStore`): OBS1's migration.

## Acceptance

- `OriginalsReaperTests`: a file in `held/` older than 30 days and a held folder over 10 GB are never reaped; the subfolder entry itself is never a candidate; top-level copies still reap as today.
- `MuteRedactorTests`: `quarantine` lands in `held/` at 0600 and backup-excluded; an unopenable final returns `.failed(.audioUnreadable)` and leaves the timeline in place; a span past the end is counted in `unmeasured_spans`; a promote failure whose restore also fails reports `restored: false`.
- `SinkDispatcherTests`: a held-back job whose WAV was not restored moves the originals copy into `held/` and reports `kept` with that path.
- `MeetingLibraryServiceTests`: a held stem exposes `heldRecordingURL`, `retryMeeting` returns the validation-prefixed error, and deleting the meeting removes the held file. A presentation test that a held row offers Show recording and no Retry.
- `cd daemon && swift build` and `swift test` green, new test counts in the commit body; `python3 scripts/truth_fences.py events` green for `at_rest_protection_failed`; `bash scripts/ci-local.sh` green.
- README.md's ADR 0016 paragraph, docs/GUIDE.md's held-back paragraph, the CONVENTIONS.md `originals/<stem>.wav` entry, and the SEC16 entry in daemon/docs/gotchas.md (which tells the owner to copy a held WAV back from `originals/`; with `held/` the redactor's byte-identical idempotency check no longer meets that copy, so the note simplifies) say that held-back and quarantined recordings live in `originals/held/`, are never reclaimed automatically, and are reachable from the row, in the same commit.
