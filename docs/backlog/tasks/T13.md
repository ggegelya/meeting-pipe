# T13: Swift test support is copy-pasted and synchronises by yield counts: seven ManualScheduler copies, three FakeDriver copies, four settle helpers, and try! in static initialisers that kill the whole run

Band origin: filed 2026-10-25 from the 2026-10-25 code-quality audit band ([code-quality audit](../../audits/2026-10-25-code-quality-audit.md) TS6, TS7, TS9, TS10). Status and priority live in this task's ToC row in [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md).

## Context

Re-checked in code on 2026-10-25.

**`ManualScheduler`, seven copies.** Five in `MeetingPipeCoreTests` (`MeetingLifecycleCoordinatorTests`, `AXLeaveButtonSignalTests`, `CallTeardownWatcherTests`, `ShareableContentSignalTests`, `ProcessAudioSignalTests`) and two in `MeetingPipeTests` (`MeetingAXWindowWatcherTests`, `CallTeardownHoldTests`). Every production `Scheduler` they stand in for is the same function type, `(TimeInterval, @escaping () -> Void) -> () -> Void`, yet the copies have drifted: `CallTeardownWatcherTests`'s is `NSLock`-guarded with `fire()` and `hasPending`, `MeetingAXWindowWatcherTests`'s records `lastDelay`, `scheduleCount` and `cancellations`, `CallTeardownHoldTests`'s is the lock-free twin of the first, and the other four keep a single `var tick: (() -> Void)?`.

**`PipelineDriver` fakes, three copies plus a no-op.** `RestoreFlowTests.FakeDriver` (31 lines, 7 funcs), `MeetingLibraryServiceTests.FakeDriver` (119 lines, 19 funcs) and `SinkDispatcherTests.FakeDriver` (55 lines, 7 funcs), 205 lines in all, plus `PipelineJobDispatcherTests.NoopDriver`. Each overrides a different subset (`runAll` is in all three; `restore`/`restorePreview`, `ask`/`summarize`/`publish`/`mergeMeetings`/`rosterEnroll`, and `finalize`/`cancelActiveRun`/`emitProgress` are each in one), relying on the protocol's `NSError` defaults for the rest.

**`settle()`, four copies, waiting by count.** `ExplicitStartLifecycleTests.settle` is `for _ in 0..<50 { await Task.yield() }`, `RestoreFlowTests.settle` and `AskModelTests.settle` are 20 yields, and `MeetingSessionControllerBranchTests.settle` is a single `await Task.yield()`. They are called 3, 16, 5 and 13 times. A yield count is a guess at how many hops the work under test takes; it passes while the guess holds and fails, or passes vacuously, when an `await` is added or the cooperative pool's width changes. `AskModelTests.pump(until:)` (poll a condition, yield, up to 500 times) already shows the right shape; `AskModelTests`' own comment explains why its `settle()` stays: proving that something did not happen needs a bounded runway.

**Main-queue drains, six named helpers.** `InputDeviceRecoveryTests.drainMainQueue` (async), `MeetingLibraryServiceTests.drainMain` and `MeetingLibraryServiceTests.drainMainQueue` (identical bodies in one file), `SinkDispatcherTests.drainMain`, and two that sleep instead of draining: `MicGateTests.drainPublishQueue` and `MuteSourceFusionTests.drain`, both `DispatchQueue.main.asyncAfter(deadline: .now() + 0.05)`. The `DispatchQueue.main.async { x.fulfill() }` idiom appears at 14 sites in total.

**`try!` in static initialisers.** Seven `private static let catalogue: MuteLabels = { try! MuteLabelsLoader.loadDefault() }()`, all in `MeetingPipeCoreTests`: two in `AXMuteButtonProbeTests`, two in `MicGateTests`, two in `MicGateAdaptersStep6Tests`, one in `MuteSourceFusionTests`. `MuteLabelsLoader.loadDefault(bundle:)` throws on a missing resource or a TOML parse failure, and a `try!` trap kills the xctest process, so one broken resource bundle reports as a crash with every later test unrun rather than as seven failing suites. Ten more `try!` sit in `MeetingPipeTests` helpers and test bodies (`SavedSearchTests` 4, `ConfigTests`, `PipelineLauncherTests.writeConfig`, `DiagnosticsLogTests`, `MeetingLibraryServiceTests`, `MeetingStoreTests`' `try! XCTUnwrap`, `LocalServerReaperTests`) with the same failure mode.

**A test that asserts nothing.** `MicGateIntegrationTests.test_start_uses_matching_adapter_for_bundle_id` builds a gate with `[zoom, teams]`, calls `gate.start(context: teamsContext, ...)` and `gate.stop()`, and has no assertion. `MicGate.start` picks one adapter per `MuteReadingSource` by `bundleIDs.contains(context.bundleID)`, so the routing the name claims is unpinned. `MuteSourceFusionTests` already has a `FakeMuteAdapter: MicGateAdapter` that could observe it.

Re-checked at filing: the `settle()` helpers wait 1, 20, 20 and 50 yields (the audit said 20 to 50), and there are four `PipelineDriver` conformers in tests, three `FakeDriver`s and a one-line `NoopDriver`.

## Scope

1. **One shared test-support folder.** A SwiftPM target `MeetingPipeTestSupport` at `daemon/Tests/TestSupport/`, a dependency of both test targets; T11 puts its scratch-dir helper in the same target, and whichever lands first creates it. It must not `import XCTest`: a plain `swift build` compiles every regular target and Command Line Tools have no XCTest. It holds `ManualScheduler` (the bare function type above, lock-guarded, recording delay, schedule count and cancellations, with `fire()`), `waitUntil(timeout:_:) async -> Bool` (poll, `Task.yield()`, deadline), one documented `yieldRunway()` for absence assertions only, and an async `drainMain()`.
2. **Doubles that need the app module** live per target, because the shared target cannot `@testable import MeetingPipe`: `Tests/MeetingPipeTests/Support/FakePipelineDriver.swift`, one configurable fake that records every call and takes a per-method result, replacing the three `FakeDriver`s and `NoopDriver`; `Tests/MeetingPipeCoreTests/Support/FakeMuteAdapter.swift`, moved out of `MuteSourceFusionTests`.
3. **Migrate.** The seven `ManualScheduler`s, the four `settle()`s (to `waitUntil` on the condition each call site is waiting for, or `yieldRunway()` where it proves absence), and the six drains, including the two 50 ms sleeps.
4. **`try!`.** The seven catalogues load in `override func setUpWithError() throws`; the other ten become `try` in throwing helpers or tests.
5. **The empty test.** `test_start_uses_matching_adapter_for_bundle_id` uses two `FakeMuteAdapter`s and asserts the Teams one started with `teamsContext` and the Zoom one never did.
6. `CONVENTIONS.md`'s test section names the support folder and the rule: wait on a condition, never a yield count.

## Explicitly not / out of scope

- Splitting `PipelineDriver` or removing its `NSError` defaults: A7 in [ARCH5](./ARCH5.md). `FakePipelineDriver` conforms to the protocol as it stands and splits with it when A7 is promoted.
- Widening the `LIBDISPATCH_COOPERATIVE_POOL_STRICT=1` step that CI8 pinned to the engine suites. `waitUntil` must pass under it; the step's filter is not changed here.
- The eight identical production `Scheduler` typealiases and the test seams compiled into release: HYG17.
- Temp-directory cleanup: T11, same support target.
- Moving `TeardownHold` into Core with an injected clock: [T8](./T8.md). If T8 lands first, `CallTeardownHoldTests` adopts the shared `ManualScheduler` there.
