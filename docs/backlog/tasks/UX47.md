# UX47: Prep card wraps its lines, admits its scroll, and stays inside its panel

Band origin: filed 2026-07-30 (owner, from a live prompt screenshot during a real Teams call: bullets sliced mid-glyph at the panel edge, "I was thinking text will be line breaked or what... and it is not visible that it is scrollable"). Status and priority live in this task's ToC row in [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md).

## Context

Three defects wearing one outfit, separated by a layout probe before any design was argued.

**Defect 1 is a bug with a probe-proven mechanism, a UX34 regression.** UX34 rehosted the card as an `NSScrollView` document, deliberately "frame-sized, not constrained". Under the layout engine a document view with `translatesAutoresizingMaskIntoConstraints` true and constraint-based subviews takes its width from its *content*, not from the frame it was handed, so in a 600pt panel the card laid itself out **879pt wide**. A throwaway XCTest built the card exactly as the panel does and measured where each label ends: the long bullets sat at maxX 773, 867, 823 against a 588 inset, every trailing constraint holding against a right edge 279pt past the glass, and the right-aligned relative-day label sat at maxX 867, meaning **the date has rendered off-panel since UX34 landed** (the owner's screenshot shows nothing to the right of the title). The configured `byTruncatingTail` never fired because the labels were never compressed. Before UX34 the card was constraint-hosted and the same labels ellipsized correctly.

**Defect 2 is a design decision the owner has overruled by eyeball.** `PrepCardView`'s header comment records CAL2's trade: single-line truncation, "the card answers what was this about, and the Library holds the full text one click away". The owner's expectation on seeing it live: lines should wrap. A recap whose every sentence dies mid-thought answers "what was this about" worse than the trade assumed.

**Defect 3 is UX34's scroll cue, falsified.** The 123pt viewport deliberately ends mid-row on the theory that a sliced row reads as "more below" (overlay scrollers are invisible at rest, so a clean row boundary reads as the end). The owner's read of the slice: "this is not okay", broken rather than inviting. The half-row bet is settled against.

A design run on 2026-07-30 put three options to the owner at 1:1 (wrap fully + fade cue; two-line clamp; honest single-line ellipsis), all three sharing the width-anchor bug fix. **Owner picked A: wrap fully, fade the bottom edge when more content is below.**

## Scope

- **Anchor the document width** (the bug, fix shared by any option): the card becomes a constraint-hosted document, width pinned to the clip view's, height pinned to the measured content height. The date comes back on-panel by construction.
- **Wrap points and actions**: `maximumNumberOfLines = 0`, word-wrapping, `preferredMaxLayoutWidth` at the real text column width. The title stays one line with a working tail ellipsis beside its date; the eyebrow and "N more in the Library" line stay one line.
- **Measure instead of counting**: `PrepCardView.layout(for:)` gains a `width:` parameter and measures each wrapped string, keeping the one-arithmetic-pass invariant (layout and `height(for:)` agree, the panel never animates to a frame the content does not fill). The row gap self-calibrates so a single-line row advances exactly the old `Metric.row`, keeping every short-card height and the existing `PrepDisclosure` arithmetic byte-identical.
- **Admit the scroll**: a bottom fade (gradient mask on the clip, so the vibrant panel material shows through rather than a wrong-colored overlay) whenever content continues below the viewport, gone when scrolled to the bottom; the overlay scroller flashes once on expand. The visibility rule is a pure function beside `PrepDisclosure.decide`, table-tested.
- **The 123pt viewport stays** as the panel's budget (UX34's cap was the right call; only its cue was wrong). Its derivation comment updates: it was derived from single-line metrics and is now simply the fixed viewport the fade reads against.

## Explicitly not in scope

The card's content and its 3+3 caps (CAL2's call, and CAL3's series-keying decides what this card shows at all; this task is the card's form, the two compose). The panel pill's 600x64 geometry. Option B's two-line clamp, recorded here as the pre-agreed retreat if wrapped scrolling annoys in practice: it is a `maximumNumberOfLines` constant away, not a rebuild.

## Acceptance

The promoted probe is the regression test: a card with real-length bullets, laid out at 600pt, has every label end inside the right inset, the date label visible and right-aligned, and at least one wrapped label taller than a single line. Pure layout tests pin: a long point grows the card while short cards keep their exact pre-wrap heights; row tops stay ordered under wrap; the fade rule's table (not scrollable, scrolled to top, at bottom, within slop of bottom). `PrepDisclosure` tests thread the width and keep their existing clamp semantics.
