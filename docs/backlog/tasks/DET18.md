# DET18: An unanswered or flickering meeting prompt loses the call: the 30 s timeout becomes a bundle-keyed skip held for the call's life, a weak starting-phase end withdraws the panel, and the hover pause freezes only the hairline

Band origin: filed 2026-11-13 (audit band 2026-11-13, see [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md)). Status and priority live in this file's header; [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) lists every task.

## Context

Sources: D018 and D064 (high, demonstrated in log), D003, D005 and D097 (medium) in [2026-11-13-recording-reliability-audit.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-recording-reliability-audit.md), with mechanism, field counts and verifier reasoning in [2026-11-13-recording-reliability-evidence.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-recording-reliability-evidence.md); stage 7 ("The offer that cannot expire") of that audit's recommended plan; F011 (high, confirmed) in [2026-11-12-independent-audit.md](../../audits/2026-11-12-independent-audit.md); and three findings in [2026-11-13-field-evidence.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-field-evidence.md): "The 30 s prompt with default skip is where meetings are lost" and "A call that starts seconds after a skipped one gets no prompt" (Sessions, prompts and end decisions) and "Late starts: 26 transcripts begin mid-conversation" (Transcripts and summaries). The lows D016, D067 and D071 are folded in below. Every mechanism was re-verified in code at HEAD `3a2da4fc` on 2026-11-13.

**What the field shows.** The work-laptop pack (114,553 events, 2026-09-29 to 2026-11-13, three build eras) holds 32 `coordinator/prompt_timeout` rows (30 `com.microsoft.teams2`, 2 `com.google.Chrome`), every one with `default_action` `skip`, 30.0 to 30.3 s after `prompt_shown`, each followed by `prompting -> idle` within 0.2 s. Of 99 prompts that stayed up long enough to answer, 61 were accepted (median 3.9 s, max 23.9 s), 32 timed out and 6 were skipped. After the 38 non-accepts, 9 hand starts followed 27 s to 1,633 s later and 29 calls were never recorded, the call UI staying visible a median 866 s. D018 counts the Teams side: 19 calls lasting 107 s to 53 min went wholly unrecorded (19,133 s, 5.3 h, an upper bound because the interval closes on the scanner's next title-only row), and at 29 of the 30 Teams timeouts process audio read true. Two calls were recorded only from minute 18 and minute 27 (`20261022-180914`, `20261105-175701`). 26 of 112 transcripts begin mid-conversation, and 6 of those follow a `prompt_timeout`. Era 3 (from 2026-11-12) has 5 prompts, all answered, so the newest build has not been tested against a timeout; the code path is unchanged.

**Mechanism 1, the timeout is a skip.** `MeetingSessionController.startPromptTimeout` reads `(self.coordinator.configStore?.defaultPromptAction ?? "skip")`, and `ConfigStore` falls back to `"skip"` too; `config.example.toml` ships `prompt_timeout_sec = 30` and `default_prompt_action = "skip"`. The default branch calls `stateMachine.abandonPrompt(source:)`, which is `cancelPromptTimeout(); recordCooldownEnd(...); armSkipLatch(...); setIdle()`, then `statusUI.setSuppressed` and `notifySkippedMeeting(source:origin: .timedOut)` (UX10's surface, kept by this task). Nothing auto-records once the latch is armed.

**Mechanism 2, the skip follows the bundle, not the call.** `SkippedMeetingLatch` keys on bundle id; `handleDiscovery` refreshes it on every scan whose confident winner is that bundle (`if coordinator.stateMachine.isSkipLatched(...) { refreshSkipLatch(...); ...; return }`), and `skipLatchGraceSec` is `skipLatchGracePolls (5) x DiscoveryScanCadence.discoveryDefault.idle`, 60 s. Its own doc comment names the blind spot: a different meeting starting in the same app within the grace inherits the latch. The field shows it: after the timeout at 2026-10-21T14:33:00Z the skipped call's UI went away at 14:56:23Z, 8 s later discovery picked Teams with mic input and a mute button (score 7), and no prompt came for 25.6 minutes; a second case on 2026-10-20 at 14:53:25Z (score 14) got none either. For a browser (D003) a strong Meet title alone wins a scan, so the latch lasts while any Meet-titled tab is active, pre-join or in-call.

**Mechanism 3, a weak end in `.starting` withdraws the panel.** While the prompt is up the engine is in `.starting`. `PromotionEngine.handleStarting` ends directly on a reliable weak end ("Meeting ended before the recorder armed ... End directly") unless END14's `veto(for:in:)` finds a live process-audio reading, and browsers emit no process audio. `MeetingSessionController.handleMeetingEndedDuringPrompt` then cancels the timeout, dismisses and goes idle, arming no backoff and logging no reason, and the idle transition's discovery refresh re-raises it. In era 1, 298 prompts were withdrawn by a same-bundle lone `shareable_content_window_gone` end and re-raised within 15 s (median gap 10.9 s, 2026-10-08T14:31:19Z to 2026-10-12T08:11:36Z), on screen about 15 to 30 ms each (D097). END14 stopped the Teams trigger, not the withdraw path, so a false end during a prompt at HEAD loops the same way. Two Chrome prompts were withdrawn after 0.18 s and 0.84 s on 2026-10-07 (D005).

**Mechanism 4, hover pauses only the hairline (F011).** `MeetingPromptWindow.setHovered` calls `dismissProgress?.setPaused(hovered)` and cancels the panel's private dismiss timer, but the timer that acts is `DetectionStateMachine.startPromptTimeout`, guarded only by `case .prompting(let src) = self.current`, with no pause hook (`SessionPromptPresenting` exposes `present` and `dismiss` only). At 30 s it dismisses and acts while the bar looks frozen, and `MeetingPromptWindow.dismiss` sets `currentSource = nil`, so a chevron-menu pick made after that hits `guard let s = currentSource else { return }` and does nothing. Hover is not logged, so the field cannot say how often this contributed.

**Lows folded in.** D016 and D067: the latch is refreshed only by a confident scan winner and lapses 60 s after the last one, so DET9's derived grace can still lapse mid-call; on the work laptop an explicit Chrome skip at 2026-09-30T13:16:45Z was re-prompted at 13:18:37Z (112 s), and D067 was found twice on the home Mac. D071 (confirmed, demonstrated): in `.starting` a reliable weak end ends the engagement at once unless process audio vetoes it (315 ended-from-starting rows in era 1, 307 within 1 s of starting, nearly all before END14's veto); browsers still have no vetoer.

Correction at filing: D064's "suppresses the call for its lifetime" is narrower at HEAD; the latch holds while discovery keeps producing confident winners and lapses 60 s after the last, so it is both too long (it swallows the next call in the same app) and too short (it can lapse mid-call). D005's tab-switch and Space-change triggers were never observed; both field withdrawals were the page retitling itself. D003's claim that a Chrome timeout lost the rest of a live call is not demonstrated (the 2026-11-11 timeout followed the title leaving the pattern). The home-Mac Zoom case of 2026-10-13 cited by the original D064 claim is not in the work-laptop pack, so this spec cites the pack's own cases instead.

## Scope

On the current engine (plan stage 7), in `MeetingSessionController` and `DetectionStateMachine`:

1. **The timeout no longer skips.** In `MeetingSessionController.startPromptTimeout`'s default branch, stop calling `abandonPrompt`: arm neither `recordCooldownEnd` nor `armSkipLatch`. Collapse the panel into a quiet one-click offer (the existing menu-bar `Suppressed (<app>)` row and the UX10 notification become "Record this <app> meeting", not a suppression), holding the prompt's own source as the attachable candidate, so a click attaches with the source and arms end detection.
2. **The offer lives for the call's microphone span.** For a native app whose `NativeLifecycleConfig` sets `usesProcessAudio` (Teams, Zoom), the offer lives while the per-scan `MeetingSourceScanner.processAudioIsRunningInput` reads true for that bundle, plus one idle poll of grace; for other native apps and for a title-only browser source, while the meeting title reads present and 60 s after. When the span ends the offer goes away without a cooldown.
3. **Skip binds to the span.** An explicit Skip (the panel's close, the chevron's Skip, force-stop while prompting) keeps suppressing, but `SkippedMeetingLatch` is anchored on the span from item 2: it clears when that span ends, so the next call in the same app prompts normally. Refreshing the latch from a process-audio reading as well as a confident winner closes D016 and D067 in the same change.
4. **A weak starting-phase end does not withdraw the panel.** In `handleMeetingEndedDuringPrompt`, a lifecycle `.ended` whose lead is not definitive (`workspaceAppTerminated`) and that arrives while the scanner still sees the bundle, or while its process audio still reads true, keeps the panel up, marked "call may have ended", and withdraws it only on a definitive signal, a microphone release, or the span lapsing. If it is withdrawn while the scanner still sees the call, back off for one idle poll instead of re-raising on the refresh.
5. **Hover pauses the real timer.** Add `pausePromptTimeout()` and `resumePromptTimeout()` to `DetectionStateMachine` (remaining time carried across, guard unchanged), expose them through `SessionPromptPresenting`, and call them from `MeetingPromptWindow.setHovered` and while the chevron menu is open. A menu pick resolves against the source held by the controller, not `MeetingPromptWindow.currentSource`, so a pick made after a dismiss still acts or is refused visibly.
6. **Log why a prompt left the screen.** Emit `coordinator/prompt_dismissed` with `bundle_id`, `reason` (`accepted`, `skipped`, `timed_out_to_offer`, `withdrawn_definitive`, `withdrawn_mic_released`, `span_lapsed`, `backoff`) and `pointer_over` (bool), and add it to `CONVENTIONS.md`'s event table.
7. **Docs in the same commit.** `docs/GUIDE.md` (the "If you ignore the panel" paragraph) and `README.md`'s `detection.default_prompt_action` entry describe the offer that stays while the call is up; the Tutorial's narration of the panel is checked against the fences in `daemon/CLAUDE.md`.

## Explicitly not

- Changing `default_prompt_action` to `record`. That is the owner's setting (the audits' no-code mitigation), not this task, and with item 1 the default `skip` stops losing calls anyway.
- Recording before consent (a provisional or pre-roll capture discarded on decline). The research companion rejects it against ADR 0016 and `PRODUCT.md` on a work laptop.
- Replacing the engine. The always-on microphone monitor and the session reducer are DET23 and DET25; this task is stage 7 on today's engine, and stage 11 deletes the cooldown and latch later only if the gate sends Teams to the reducer.
- `abandonPrompt` seeding no candidate for a hand start inside the first seconds (D098): the [DET13](./DET13.md) amendment from this band.
- The source-less recording ended by a foreign browser engage (field, 2026-11-11): END19.
- The browser's single end signal and the title-only start: [DET20](./DET20.md) and DET17.
- [SEC24](./SEC24.md) (P3, the consent notice): untouched.

## Acceptance

- `DetectionStateMachineTests`: a prompt timeout with action `skip` leaves `isCoolingDown` and `isSkipLatched` false for the bundle and keeps the source as the attachable candidate; an explicit Skip still latches, and the latch clears when the span-end input fires, so a second same-bundle sighting after it prompts.
- `MeetingSessionControllerTests`: (a) the 2026-10-21 shape, timeout, the skipped call's sighting stops, then a full-signature sighting (mic and mute) 8 s later produces a prompt; (b) a full-signature sighting after a both-absent poll gets a prompt; (c) a weak `.ended` (window gone, bundle still sighted) during `.prompting` keeps the panel and emits no `prompting -> idle`, while `workspaceAppTerminated` withdraws it; (d) a withdraw while the scanner still sees the call does not re-present on the immediate refresh; (e) a menu pick delivered after the panel was dismissed by the timeout acts on the held source.
- A hover test with an injected clock: hovering at 20 s and leaving at 50 s fires the timeout at 60 s, not 30 s; the chevron menu open pauses it the same way.
- `coordinator/prompt_dismissed` is emitted on every exit with the reasons above and is in `CONVENTIONS.md`; `python3 scripts/truth_fences.py events` green.
- `cd daemon && swift build` and `swift test` green, N new tests named in the commit body; `bash scripts/ci-local.sh` green, the Tutorial fences included.
