## Context

The END class grows one incident at a time. `git log` on `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle` shows 11 END commits from TECH-END1 (2026-06-10) to END14 (2026-10-12), each adding a rule: corroboration, the correlated pair, the teardown oracle, a started watcher, the stale-log removal, the cap veto, the live veto. [END9](./END9.md) to [END14](./END14.md) are all `partial`, waiting on the owner's field confirmation, and [END15](./END15.md) (2026-10-22) is the next shape. Each fix has been proven by the meeting after it.

**Premise correction.** The review says there is no replay harness. There is one, and its limits are the defect. `DetectionCorpusTests` replays the 29 scenarios in `daemon/Tests/MeetingPipeCoreTests/Fixtures/detection-corpus/INDEX.json` (26 `promotion`, 3 `micgate`) through `PromotionEngine`. C6-FINISH (2026-07-13, [q6-final.md](../q6-final.md)) added the 20 real `teams_native_*` traces through [`scripts/extract_detection_trace.py`](../../../scripts/extract_detection_trace.py). Three things stop it catching an END regression:

1. **The real traces are distilled.** The extractor reads `lifecycle.*` verdict rows and emits "the minimal signal timeline that reproduces the real VERDICT sequence the engine logged", not the raw `signal` rows. A trace can only say what the engine of that day decided, so a false end replays as the false end it was, and the raw flicker that caused it (END15's `ax_leave_button_state healthy -> invalid` then `process_audio_is_running_input true -> false`, six seconds apart) is not in any trace.
2. **It drives the engine only.** The hold END9 built lives in the host. `MeetingSessionController.TeardownHold` (`startedAt`, `timer`, `vetoLogged`), `beginTeardownHold` (re-entrant, keeps the first stamp), `consultTeardownOracle(now:)` on a 1 Hz `Timer.scheduledTimer`, `releaseTeardownHold`, and the trim decision `pendingEndTrimAt = heldFor >= 1.0 ? hold.startedAt : nil`. The corpus models the host's re-walk with a `confirm_provisional_end` pseudo-event, which is the exact path END9 replaced with the hold. `CallTeardownOracle.decide` is pure and table-tested; the state machine around it is not in Core, and `beginTeardownHold` and `releaseTeardownHold` read `Date()` directly.
3. **No incident is in it.** Every real trace was extracted on 2026-07-13, before END9 shipped (2026-08-20). Not one END9, END12, END13, END14 (217 window-gone ends on 2026-10-08) or END15 shape exists as a trace, though each spec quotes the raw rows that would make one.

## Scope

1. **Hold policy into Core.** A pure type in `MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle` (working name `TeardownHoldPolicy`) owns what `TeardownHold` and its three functions decide: open, re-entry keeping the first stamp, consult at an injected `now`, release on revert, the cap-veto log-once flag, and the trim point (held at least 1 s). The host keeps the `Timer`, the watcher snapshot reads, `Log`, and the HUD calls, and drives the policy with its clock. No behaviour change.
2. **A raw trace format.** The corpus gains an `engine: "lifecycle"` trace kind carrying raw signal events plus the host inputs over time: teardown snapshots (`teardown_at`, `available`, `log_written_at`), live-call signal readings, and the re-walk result (`rewalk_found`). Expected outcome is `ended_at` with `trim_at`, or `no_end`. A replay driver runs `PromotionEngine`, the policy and `CallTeardownOracle.decide` on a virtual clock.
3. **A raw extractor mode.** `extract_detection_trace.py --raw --from <iso> --to <iso>` emits a lifecycle trace from the `signal`, `lifecycle` and `coordinator` rows in that window, titles dropped and pid 1234, and `redact_detection_trace.py --check` accepts the new keys. Stdlib only, like the rest of the script.
4. **Seed the incidents.** At least one raw trace each for END9 (empty re-walk), END12 (stale log), END13 (cap with a live signal, 2026-09-04), END14 (lone window-gone with Teams holding the mic) and END15 (mic switch, 2026-10-22, both signal orders). The expected outcome is the correct one, `no_end`. A trace for a defect still open (END15 today) carries `"known_defect": "END15"`, and the harness asserts the current wrong outcome for it, so the fix has to flip the flag in the same commit.
5. **The rule.** The corpus `README.md` states that an END or REC change lands with this replay green and, for an incident fix, a new raw trace of that incident.

## Explicitly not / out of scope

- Any change to when a call ends. The policy inversion is END17, and this harness is what END17 is verified against, so T8 lands first.
- Re-extracting or deleting the 20 distilled traces; they stay as characterization of genuine ends.
- The idle-stop backstop, which [C7](../q6-final.md) closed as an accepted corpus gap.
- A freeze on filing new END tasks while END9 to END14 await confirmation: DOC17 carries the WIP limit.
