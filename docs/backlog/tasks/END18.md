# END18: The end engine decides on edges, not current levels: a healthy Leave re-arm never reverts a hold, a same-class revert strands a hold after the mic released, and the lone dwell counts from the first doubt

Band origin: filed 2026-11-13 (audit band 2026-11-13, see [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md)). Status and priority live in this file's header; [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) lists every task.

## Context

Sources: F002 and G006 in [2026-11-12-independent-audit.md](../../audits/2026-11-12-independent-audit.md); D027, D029 and D104 in [2026-11-13-recording-reliability-audit.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-recording-reliability-audit.md) with evidence in [2026-11-13-recording-reliability-evidence.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-recording-reliability-evidence.md); and two field findings in [2026-11-13-field-evidence.md](../../audits/2026-11-13-field-evidence.md), "A Leave-button reading that stays or returns healthy overrides a mic release" (Sessions) and "Era A: recordings cut after 22 to 87 s by an unconfirmed window-gone end right after a rescue that did nothing" (Transcripts). Every code claim below was re-read at HEAD `3a2da4fc` on 2026-11-13.

**The class.** The signals report levels (the Leave control is there, Teams holds the mic, the window is on screen) but deliver them as edges: `AXLeaveButtonSignal._evaluate` returns `.steady` when `lastState == state`, `ProcessAudioSignal` and `ShareableContentSignal` emit only on change. `PromotionEngine` keeps each kind's last reading in `SignalStates`, then decides most things on the event that just arrived rather than on those readings. Three defects follow, one per decision.

**1. END8's fix does not hold: the rescue's healthy Leave never reaches the engine (F002, G006, D027).** END8 (done, commit `11bac7b4`, 2026-07-12, spec in [q6-final.md](../q6-final.md)) shipped the revert of a window-gone hold by the AX rescue's healthy Leave button. The promise is still written in three places: `MeetingSessionController.rescueProvisionalEnd`'s doc ("re-arm on it so its healthy baseline reverts the hold to `.inMeeting` (END8)"), the `.live` arm of `PromotionEngine.handleEndingProvisional` ("END8: the AX rescue's healthy Leave button reverts a window-gone lead"), and ADR [0017](../../decisions/0017-hold-long-trim-later-end-detection.md)'s revert bullet. The path is `rescueProvisionalEnd` to `MeetingLifecycleCoordinator.armLeaveButton` to `NativeLifecycleAdapter.armLeaveButton` to `AXLeaveButtonSignal.armIfNeeded`, whose `_armIfNeeded` starts `if let current = element, probe(current) == .healthy { return }`. With the Leave button armed and healthy all along, nothing is emitted, so no `.live` arrives and the revert never fires. `tick` does not look at a live same-class reading already in `states` either. The tests split the two halves: `PromotionEngineTests.test_window_gone_provisional_reverts_when_ax_leave_returns_healthy` injects the `.live` by hand, and `AXLeaveButtonSignalTests.test_armIfNeeded_is_noop_when_already_armed` pins the no-op ("Already-armed signal absorbs the late-arm", which is right for the late-arm race it was written for). No test drives the rescue through the real signal into the engine. [END14](./END14.md)'s spec recorded the no-emit on 2026-10-12 and added a process-audio veto beside it rather than fixing it.

Field, era 1 (work laptop, before the 2026-10-12 rebuild, debounce 2 s): the exact signature three times, `ending_provisional` led by `shareable_content_window_gone`, then `ax_leave_button_rearmed` and `lifecycle_provisional_end_rescued` with `leave_buttons_found` 1, then `lifecycle`/`ended` with `confirmed_by` [] 2.0 to 2.1 s after the provisional: 2026-10-08T14:38:37.386Z (stem `20261008-173712`, cut at 87.0 s), 2026-10-09T09:17:28.281Z (`20261009-121708`, 22.0 s) and 2026-10-09T14:31:32.182Z (`20261009-173127`, 32.5 s). The last readings before each were mic held and Leave healthy; each recording ends mid-sentence, a new prompt followed 65 to 68 s later, and all three slivers still got a summary. 0 of 16 rescues in the pack ever produced a Leave live edge.

Correction at filing: the field report reads the absence of this signature after 2026-10-12 as the build stopping it. It is masked, not fixed. In eras 2 and 3 there were 0 window-gone-led provisionals because all 136 window-gone ends were absorbed by END14's process-audio veto (`lifecycle`/`end_vetoed`). At HEAD the defect is live wherever that veto cannot fire: Webex and Slack (`NativeLifecycleAdapter.init` sets `processAudio = nil` when `config.usesProcessAudio` is false), and Teams or Zoom while process audio is unresolved or not live. There a call window kept off screen (minimised, another Space; `ShareableContentSignal` enumerates on-screen windows only) for 720 s ends the recording on the lone dwell and trims the held tail. Under a non-regulated workflow the tail stays in the WAV and is only left out of the transcript (restorable); under the regulated gate it is cut. Also corrected: the audit's fourth era-1 case, 2026-10-09T09:18:49Z (`20261009-121844`), is a different path, the Leave control read invalid and `ax_leave_button_invalid` confirmed it, so it is not evidence for this defect.

**2. A same-class revert strands a hold after the mic released (D104, field Sessions).** In `handleEndingProvisional`'s `.live` arm the hold reverts when `event.kind.evidenceClass == leading.evidenceClass || returning || event.kind.vetoesLoneEnd`, whatever else `states` already holds. After the revert the phase is `.inMeeting`, `hasPendingEndDeadline` is false so the coordinator's tick stops, and because the other signals emit only on change, the readings that already went ended will never be sent again. `SilenceAutoStopPolicy.shouldAutoStop` returns false for a native source while the lifecycle is live, so the idle backstop stands down too. The recording then runs until the Leave probe flips invalid again, the app quits, a storage stop fires, or the owner presses Stop. The probe makes the healthy reading easy to get: `AXLeaveButtonSignal.defaultProbe` returns `status == .invalidUIElement ? .invalid : .healthy`, so every other AX status, including a read against a window that is tearing down, reads healthy.

Field, era 3 (the 2026-11-12 build, same code as HEAD for both files), Zoom stem `20261112-170131`: Leave invalid at 15:19:45.186Z opened a hold (`ending_provisional` led by `ax_leave_button_invalid`, `lifecycle_end_held` with `live_signal` `call_is_up`); process audio went false at 15:19:49.322Z; the window went at 15:19:50.967Z; the Leave button read healthy again at 15:19:53.344Z (reason `health_poll`, no `ax_leave_button_rearmed` or `ax_leave_button_self_armed` near it); `lifecycle`/`in_meeting` at 15:19:53.350Z and `lifecycle_end_hold_released` (`held_sec` 8.17) at .356Z; the owner's own Stop at 15:19:54.638Z, with no `lifecycle`/`ended` before it. One of 30 invalid-to-healthy transitions pack-wide matches this signature. The unbounded run is code-derived because the owner stopped 1.3 s later.

Correction at filing: the field finding's era-1 Teams cases (`20261006-163051`, a mic-led provisional open 562 s until `auto_stop_silence`, tail 550 s; `20261008-163049`, 63 s until a hand stop; in both the last Leave reading was healthy after a rescue) ran on the pre-END17 engine, where a mic lead needed a cross-class corroborator. At HEAD a hold whose Leave simply stays healthy is bounded by the lone dwell (720 s, tail trimmed), which is END17's accepted cost, so the HEAD defect is the revert on a returning healthy reading, not the stay-healthy shape.

**3. The lone dwell counts from the first doubt (D029, code-derived, not seen in the field).** `tick` computes `let loneHeld = now.timeIntervalSince(startedAt) >= loneDwell`, where `startedAt` is when the hold opened. The live-mic veto (`states.contains(where: { $0.key.vetoesLoneEnd && $0.value.state == .live })`) only pauses it. An ax-leave lead is not `yieldsToLiveVeto`, so it opens a hold even while Teams holds the mic (END13's 2026-09-04 shape, named in `vetoesLoneEnd`'s doc). If that hold survives 12 minutes under the live mic, the first momentary mic release (a device switch, DET8's object churn) removes the veto, and the next tick, `MeetingLifecycleCoordinator.defaultTickInterval` 0.25 s later, confirms with `lone_dwell`. That is the END15 mic-switch chop again, in the one state END17's 60 s pair dwell does not cover.

Related: [END14](./END14.md) (partial) carries a 2026-11-13 amendment on the same function, `tick`'s pair counting (F031, G024: a class counted as ended while a sibling reads live, and stale stamps); land the two together or rebase one on the other. [END15](./END15.md) (partial) and [END17](./END17.md) (partial, the hold) are the designs this corrects; [END10](./END10.md) records the rescue working when Leave was not yet armed. [END21](./END21.md) owns the held-end trim point and stop routes. [END26](./END26.md) is the gated reducer switch for Teams; if its GATE moves Teams ends to the reducer, that switch supersedes this task for Teams only, and every other app still needs this.

## Scope

All decision changes are in `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle/PromotionEngine.swift` and stay pure; the probe and rescue changes are the only host-side edits.

1. **A revert reads the other classes (D104).** In `handleEndingProvisional`'s `.live` arm, a same-class or returning reading reverts the hold only when no other evidence class has a `.ended` reading stamped inside the hold (`at >= startedAt`). When one has, the hold stays open and the reading moves the lone-dwell anchor (item 4) instead. A vetoer's `.live` (process audio) still reverts unconditionally, as END14 requires. The coordinator logs the declined revert as `lifecycle`/`end_revert_declined` (`bundle_id`, `leading_signal`, `reading`, `ended_classes`), drained the way `takeVeto()` is.
2. **The rescue reaches the engine (F002, G006, D027).** Give `AXLeaveButtonSignal` a rescue entry, reached from `rescueProvisionalEnd` through `MeetingLifecycleCoordinator` and `NativeLifecycleAdapter` beside `armLeaveButton`, that re-emits `onChange(.healthy)` when the armed element probes healthy, so the healthy baseline arrives as a `.live` the engine can act on under item 1. `armIfNeeded`'s late-arm no-op stays as it is. And in `tick`, refuse the lone dwell while a kind in the lead's evidence class reads `.live` in `states` and no other class has ended inside the hold, so the revert no longer depends on an emit alone.
3. **A three-valued Leave probe (D104).** `AXLeaveButtonSignal.defaultProbe` reads `.healthy` only on `.success`, `.invalid` on `.invalidUIElement`, and a new `State.unknown` on every other status. `_evaluate` treats `.unknown` as no reading: it keeps `lastState`, emits nothing and never counts as a return. That keeps the existing intent (a TCC hiccup from healthy does not promote) and stops a teardown read from passing as a healthy return.
4. **The lone dwell runs from the last liveness reading (D029).** Replace `startedAt` in `tick`'s `loneHeld` with a lone-dwell anchor carried in `.endingProvisional`: set when the hold opens, moved to the moment a vetoer stops reading live inside the hold (its `.ended` stamp), and moved by item 1's declined revert. So the lone dwell needs the mic to stay released for the whole `loneDwell`, and `startedAt` keeps meaning only where the held tail starts (END21's trim point is unchanged here).
5. **ADR 0017's revert bullet** and the stale comments in `rescueProvisionalEnd` and `handleEndingProvisional` say what the code now does, in the same commit.
