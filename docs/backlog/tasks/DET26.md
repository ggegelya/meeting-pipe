# DET26: Bind offers, declines, hand starts and relaunches to a microphone session instead of a bundle, for Teams and then Zoom (gated)

Band origin: filed 2026-11-13 (audit band 2026-11-13, see [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md)). Status and priority live in this file's header; [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) lists every task.

## Context

Source: stage 11 of the staged plan in the [recording reliability audit](../../audits/2026-11-13-recording-reliability-audit.md) ("Start switch for Teams, then Zoom if Z1 passed (gated)"), findings D065 and D073, with lows D006, D023, D024 and D026 folded in. Full claims, field rows and verifier notes are in the [evidence file](../../audits/2026-11-13-recording-reliability-evidence.md). The field pack is the work laptop, 2026-09-29 to 2026-11-13, 74 recordings over three build eras (A before 2026-10-12, B to 2026-11-12, C after). Re-verified in code at HEAD `3a2da4fc` on 2026-11-13; every citation below is a symbol at that commit.

**Why P3 and gated.** The plan sequences this after the GATE (a written decision, kept in [END26](./END26.md), on whether Teams moves to the session reducer at all) and after stage 10, the Teams end switch (END26 itself), and it depends on the always-on microphone ledger ([DET23](./DET23.md)) and the shadow reducer ([DET25](./DET25.md)) that define what a session is. Zoom follows only if bar Z1 in [DET24](./DET24.md) passes. If the GATE keeps Teams on the patched engine, this task stays parked and the interim fixes on the old engine (listed under Explicitly not) carry the field load. The owner may promote it by editing the header once the gate is passed.

**D065, a hand start with no detected source never adopts the meeting.** `MeetingSessionController.startManual` attaches only to a live suppressed candidate: `if let candidate = coordinator.stateMachine.liveSuppressedCandidate() { recordAttached(...) } else { beginRecording(source: nil, ...) }`. With `source` nil, `beginRecording`'s attach path (MicGate engage, END11's explicit-start lifecycle engage, the Leave arm) never runs. Nothing later gives the recording an identity: `handleDiscovery` and `handleMeetingStarted` both open with `guard coordinator.stateMachine.isAcceptingPrompts else { return }`, and since PERF17 `MeetingDiscoveryWatcher` does not scan outside `.idle` at all ("no scan runs, while the mic-busy sampling ... carry on"). Field, demonstrated: 4 of 74 recordings were hand starts with `bundle_id` manual, stems `20261012-103055` (A, after 2 vanished prompts), `20261021-103054` (B, 10.3 s after a skip), `20261106-172834` (B, 9.0 s after a timeout) and `20261111-155951` (B, no prompt at all). None has a `lifecycle`/`in_meeting` row; 3 stopped with no cause row (overruns past the last Teams sighting of 14 s, about 5 min after a `silence_notified`, and 15 s); the fourth was stopped at 2026-11-11T14:14:28Z by an unrelated Chrome lifecycle end, which is [END19](./END19.md)'s defect. The audit also found why the source was missing: 2 of 2 hand starts within 12 s of a skip or timeout were source-less, 6 of 6 at 25 s or later attached, because nothing seeds the candidate at the abandon ([DET13](./DET13.md)'s 2026-11-13 amendment, D098).

**D073, a relaunch during a call forgets everything.** Every start-side decision lives in memory: `RepromptCooldown` is `private var lastEnd: [String: Date]`, `SkippedMeetingLatch` is `private var lastSeen: [String: Date]`, and END17's way back is `MeetingSessionController.resumeCandidate`, a plain `var`. The only thing that survives a relaunch is `RecordingManifest` (`<stem>.recovery.json`), which holds `summary_mode` and the start-time `meta` and nothing about the call; `OrphanRecordingRecovery` re-routes the orphan by it but writes no `resumed_from` link and seeds no resume. So after a relaunch mid-recording the first half is recovered as a standalone meeting, and the call that is still running meets a fresh prompt whose timeout defaults to skip after 30 s (`ConfigDefaults.promptTimeoutSec` 30, `default_prompt_action` read as `?? "skip"` in `ConfigStore`). After a relaunch mid-skip the latch is gone and the skipped call prompts again. Correction at filing: the relaunch half is code-derived and contested in the field, not seen once. The one candidate is Chrome on 2026-09-30 (build A): `user_skipped` 13:16:45Z, a launch at 13:18:06Z, `prompt_shown` for the same bundle and pid at 13:18:37Z, `prompt_timeout` at 13:19:07Z; the evidence file books it under D073, but D026's verifier found the lifecycle had re-engaged 22 s before that launch, and no prompt came within 60 s of a native timeout or skip across the pack's 12 launches.

**D026, suppression keyed by bundle (holds at HEAD; RepromptCooldown is a `[String: Date]`).** The latch's own doc comment names the blind spot ("the latch keys on bundle id, not a meeting-instance id"). Field, demonstrated on Teams: 2 calls (81 s and 25.6 min, picks at 2026-10-20T14:53:25Z and 2026-10-21T14:56:31Z) began inside the previous unanswered call's 60 s latch grace and were never prompted, because each confident scan refreshed the latch; 4 back-to-back calls (2026-10-01, 10-22, 11-05, 11-06) had their prompt held 10 to 71 s by the previous recording's cooldown and all 4 then timed out. At HEAD END17's 120 s `ResumeAfterEnd` window would merge those 4 into the previous recording under its workflow instead of prompting them late (D021's shape, inferred in the audit), which is a different wrong answer, not a fix.

**D006, the same keying on a browser (code-derived, not seen).** The cooldown is stamped in three places, all by bundle: `resetAfterStop` (`coordinator.stateMachine.recordCooldownEnd(bundleID: bid)`), `DetectionStateMachine.abandonPrompt` (`recordCooldownEnd` plus `armSkipLatch`), and `beginRecording`'s failed-start catch (`cooldown_armed`). For a browser every Meet tab shares one bundle, so the next call in the same browser inherits the previous call's cooldown or latch.

**D024, a hand Stop mid-call arms only the cooldown (holds at HEAD, not seen in the pack).** `toggleManual` in `.recording` calls `stopRecording`, whose tail `resetAfterStop` arms the 60 s cooldown and nothing else; `resumeCandidate` is written only in `handleMeetingEnded`. So once the minute lapses, the next idle scan prompts the very call the owner just chose to stop recording. The session model marks that session declined instead.

**D023, no Accessibility grant means no native detection (confirmed in code; not in play on the work laptop).** `MeetingSourceScanner.collectSignals` reads `processAudioIsRunningInput` outside the `axTrusted` gate (DET12) but every other native signal inside it, and `MeetingSourceScorer.isConfidentNativeMeeting` needs `signals.callingControlsToolbar || distinctSignalCount(signals) >= 2`, so a lone audio read never clears the bar; DET1's catch-all `plausibleBundles` excludes natives on purpose. Correction at filing: the suggested interim, showing a missing grant as detection-off, already ships: DET11's `StatusBarModel.accessibilityOffTitle` ("Accessibility off - meetings are not detected"), the `accessibility_denied_at_startup` event and `notifyAccessibilityBlocked()`. What remains is a consequence of this task: once sessions open on the microphone holder, an AX-denied Mac does detect calls, and that copy becomes false.

**What the plan's field read says about the cost.** 6 Teams calls lost or delayed to bundle-keyed suppression in 38 active days (D026), 4 source-less hand starts with no automatic end (D065), and a standing habit of starting by hand: the audit's stage 11 exit bar is that at least 90 percent of hand starts end up bound to a session.

## Scope

All of it lands only for the families the GATE switched (Teams first; Zoom after Z1), behind the same flag END26 uses, so rollback is one switch. The session is the one DET25's `MicSession` reducer defines (a family's microphone hold from open to close after the grace G), fed by DET23's monitor.

1. **Session-bound offer and decline.** One offer per session. Skip, the prompt timeout's skip and a hand Stop all mark the session declined, stored on the session, never on the bundle (D006, D024, D026). A new session in the same app gets its own offer even seconds later. `RepromptCooldown`, `SkippedMeetingLatch` with its grace polls, the suppressed and cooldown candidates (`DetectionStateMachine.noteSuppressedCandidate`, `liveSuppressedCandidate`) and the bundle-keyed `ResumeAfterEnd` stop being consulted for switched families; their code stays until stage 14 deletes it.
2. **Rejoin with fresh routing.** A drop shorter than G is the same session and keeps its recording; a session that opens after the previous one closed is a new session with its own offer and its own workflow resolution, never the previous recording's workflow or decline.
3. **Hand-start adoption.** A hand start (hotkey, menu row, `meetingpipe://record`, the start-late notification) binds to the open session if there is one. With none open it records unbound and adopts the first session that opens: set the source, engage the lifecycle and MicGate, arm Leave, rewrite the recovery manifest's `meta` with the adopted source, and log `coordinator`/`session_adopted` (bundle id, seconds since start). Adoption reads the monitor, not a discovery scan, so PERF17's idle-only scan gate stays. Workflow resolution at adoption follows the owner's ADR ruling (scope item 7); the default is to keep the start-time workflow and record the adopted source only, so routing never changes under a running recording without a rule saying so.
4. **The scanner as an offer-only second opener.** A confident AX winner with no microphone session may raise an offer, never auto-record and never end anything; it is how a call that holds no microphone (joined with audio off) still gets asked.
5. **Relaunch journal.** Persist the open session (family, pid, opened at, decision, the recording stem) in `RecordingManifest` at each change. At launch, if the journal names an open session whose family still holds the microphone, resume without a prompt into a new stem whose sidecar carries `resumed_from` naming the orphan, under the same workflow and summary mode, and log `coordinator`/`session_resumed_after_relaunch`; a declined session stays declined across the relaunch; a session whose microphone is gone is closed and the orphan is recovered as today. Coordinate the manifest keys with [REC21](./REC21.md), which hardens the same file.
6. **Always-record as a visible, undoable list.** An Always app's session records at open with consent route `always` in the ledger. The list itself, visible and editable in Preferences, is [UX97](./UX97.md); this task binds that list to sessions and must not land before UX97 does.
7. **An ADR for the consent choices.** Record the owner's answers to the plan's questions that this task turns into behaviour: always-record for Teams or not; whether a session reopened within two minutes of a recorded call records at once or offers with Continue preselected; whether back-to-back calls joined within a minute should fuse or split; and the adoption routing rule in item 3. Cross-reference [SEC24](./SEC24.md)'s consent posture.
8. **Docs in the same commits.** `docs/GUIDE.md`'s prompt and back-to-back paragraphs (the `Suppressed (<app>)` reading, "stops asking about that meeting app for about a minute", the Record this `<app>` meeting row), `README.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` (Suppressed candidate, the cooldown), `CONVENTIONS.md` for the new events, and `StatusBarModel.accessibilityOffTitle`'s copy if an AX-denied Mac now detects calls (D023).

- Audio captured before consent: never built, per the plan.
- Detection on an AX-denied Mac beyond what the session opener gives for free (D023): no separate work.
