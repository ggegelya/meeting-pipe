# AI15: spike, listen-only routing by measured owner talk share

Band origin: filed 2026-11-13 from the product-direction band ([report](../../research/2026-11-12-product-direction.md), idea I26, "Listen-only shape: lectures and all-hands get an outline, not invented action items"). Child of epic [EPIC1](./EPIC1.md). `/spike` shaped. Parked: promote by editing the header. Status and priority live in this file's header; [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) lists every task.

## The one question

**What share of recordings has the owner speaking under 2% of the time, and are the actions those recordings produce mostly not the owner's?**

## Context and what changed

AI5 (automatic meeting-type classification) closed NO-GO on 2026-07-23 at 45.6% labeller agreement ([spike](../../spikes/ai5-meeting-type-classification.md), row in [q6-final.md](../q6-final.md)); its own conclusion was that the gate needs a better input, not a better classifier, and it never measured channel talk share. Talk-time coaching stays rejected. What changed: this is a physical measurement on the owner's own channel used to route a schema, with no chart and no score. DIAR6 (a quiet mic merges 30 to 39% remote speech into `me`) means the measure is mic-channel voice activity counted only where the system channel is quiet or clearly lower, not the diarized share. UX74's mtime-derived durations mean the denominator is the audio frame count. Same channel hypothesis as [ASR7](./ASR7.md).

## Pre-registered verdict thresholds (copied from the report)

A script over the library computes owner talk share per recording as defined above, prints the distribution in buckets, the number under 2%, and how many actions and decisions those produced. The owner then marks the actions from the 10 most recent listen-only recordings real-for-me or not.

| Verdict | Measured | Next |
|---|---|---|
| **GO** | At least 8% of recordings under 2% owner talk share AND at least 80% of their actions not real for the owner | Outline schema (topics with timestamps, claims, references, asks of me), shown on the meeting and reversible in one click |
| **MARGINAL** | The share holds but 50 to 80% not real | Keep the meeting schema; add only a visible "you did not speak" marker |
| **NO-GO** | Under 3% of recordings, or more than half of those actions real | Close |

## Scope

The GO build is one routing key (owner talk share under 2%) selecting an outline schema per WF7's per-workflow sections, shown on the meeting with a one-click reroute; MARGINAL builds only the marker.

## Owner walkthrough

Run the counts command, then mark the actions of the 10 most recent listen-only recordings real-for-me or not (about 10 minutes). The READ line prints GO, MARGINAL or NO-GO.

## Explicitly not

A permanent meeting-type field (AI5 stands); any talk-time view; a second schema before ARCH10 has one writer for per-meeting files. Its main payoff shrinks if the Facts rail is removed ([FEAT22](./FEAT22.md), DV5); re-check before promoting.
