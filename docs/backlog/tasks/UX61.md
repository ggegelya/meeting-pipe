# UX61: batch "Merge…" fires on click with no confirmation, and its ellipsis promises a surface it never opens

Filed 2026-08-03 by the audit band. Proposed high, downgraded to medium by an independent skeptic, which established that the merged artifacts are supersets and the fragments are recoverable. The narrowed claim is filed.

## What is wrong

- `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/BatchActionsPane.swift:174` is `Button("Merge…") { Task { await runMerge() } }`, invoking the action directly from the button.
- `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/BatchActionsPane.swift:537-552` is the whole of `runMerge`: `MeetingMergeEligibility.decide` then `await libraryModel.mergeMeetings(...)`. No alert, no confirmationDialog, no gate.
- The same file has exactly two confirmations, `:44` ("Move N meetings to Trash?") and `:58` ("Re-transcribe N meetings?"), and neither is the merge.
- `design/README.md:97-99` makes the ellipsis meaningful in this repo, and this one opens nothing.

What the click does: `pipeline/src/mp/merge_meetings.py:228-229` overwrites the primary's transcript via `atomic_write_text`, `:230` replaces its audio in place with `os.replace`, and `:236-237` regenerates the summary through a paid engine call and republishes the already-published page. The other N-1 meetings go to the Trash.

`daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/MeetingMergeEligibility.swift:53-64` requires only two or more done meetings with audio, one workflow id and one privacy posture. Nothing requires them to be fragments of one call, so **any two finished same-workflow meetings offer Merge**, and the owner's default `Personal` workflow holds 117 meetings.

## What the skeptic established, recorded

The merged audio and transcript are supersets of the primary's own (`merge_meetings.py:180`, `:71-73`), and the fragments go to the recoverable system Trash (`MeetingLibraryService.swift:915`). The only content actually lost is the primary's previous `<stem>.summary.json` with its local edits and action-done ticks.

The "unconfirmed while its less-destructive neighbours confirm" framing was also partly selective: `Re-summarize all` (`:234`) and `Republish all` (`:285`) are unconfirmed too. That makes this a UX-consistency and labelling defect on a never-executed path rather than a data-loss defect, which is why it is medium.

## Scope

Add a confirmation shaped like the two already in the file, naming what is about to be lost: which meeting becomes the primary, that its recording, transcript and summary are replaced **in place** and not moved to the Trash, that the other N-1 meetings go to the Trash, and that the summary is regenerated and republished, which is a paid call and a page rewrite when the workflow's backend is cloud.

Route it through `LibraryDialogs` so the copy has one home, the way the Trash confirm does.

Worth deciding in the same pass, since the skeptic surfaced it: whether `Re-summarize all` and `Republish all` deserve confirmations too. Both spend money and rewrite published pages. The answer may be no, but it should be recorded rather than left as an accident of which action got a gate first.

## Acceptance

Suite green with new tests. The merge cannot run without an explicit confirm. The confirmation copy is a pure function pinned by a test, in the shape `WorkflowDeletion`'s copy already uses. The ellipsis on the button is now honest.
