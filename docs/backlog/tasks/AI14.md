# AI14: Spike: does a 2025-generation multilingual embedder beat multilingual-e5-small on the owner's Ukrainian and English library search, at a latency the library can carry

Band origin: filed 2026-11-12 from the dependency, platform and AI-model upgrade review (owner approved "File all proposed"). Status and priority live in this file's header; [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) lists every task.

## Context

`embed_index.DEFAULT_EMBED_MODEL` is `"mlx-community/multilingual-e5-small-mlx"`: XLM-RoBERTa, 384-dimensional, about 120 MB, MIT, published 2024-01-11. It was chosen in AI2 for en/uk strength at a small size.

Two newer multilingual embedders have MLX builds on mlx-community (checked 2026-11-12):
- Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B: 8-bit (2025-08) and mxfp8 (2026-02) builds, Apache-2.0;
- EmbeddingGemma-300m: 4-bit and bf16 builds (2025-09), Gemma licence.

The installed mlx-embeddings 0.1.0 (the latest release) ships `models/qwen3.py` and `models/gemma3_text.py` beside `xlm_roberta.py`. So either loads through `MLXEmbedder` with no dependency change.

**A swap means a full re-index**, since vectors of different dimension and space cannot mix. AI13's generation manifest makes a re-index atomic.

## Scope

A `/spike`. Pre-register the bars before any score:
- retrieval quality on a fixed query set over the owner's corpus, half Ukrainian and half English, judged by the owner (recall@5 against e5-small);
- per-query embed latency and full-index build time, against AI2's measured latency budget for the library;
- resident memory.

Then deliver a verdict: swap (and which model), or keep e5-small with the reason.

## Explicitly not

- Not the speaker-embedding model, which is FluidAudio's and moves voiceprints (ASR6).
- Not changing the dependency set.
- Not shipping a re-index in the spike.

## Acceptance

- A committed spike doc under `docs/spikes/` with the bars above the scores, per-model results and a named verdict.
- If the verdict is to swap, a follow-up task is filed for the default change and re-index. The spike changes no repo code.
