**This reverses part of DET5.** Scope item 1 narrows the walk admissions DET5 added on 2026-07-12 (`isLoneNative` and `isFrontmost` in `shouldWalkControlAX`, its walk-gate decouple, archived in [q6-final.md](../q6-final.md)), which exist so a native call whose window was renamed still gets its control walk. ## Context

**What PERF6 fixed.** The 2026-06-30 idle-energy finding put MeetingPipe's idle CPU at about 8 to 13% (at the then 3 s discovery poll) in the three recursive AX tree walks in `MeetingSourceScanner.collectSignals`. PERF6 moved the quiet poll to 12 s (`DiscoveryScanCadence.discoveryDefault = DiscoveryScanCadence(active: 3, idle: 12)`) and gated the walks behind the pure `MeetingSourceScanner.shouldWalkControlAX`, then `titleMatch || processAudioActive`. Its ship note: "an idle app reports default signals, scored identically to walked and found nothing".

**How the gate reopened**, re-verified at HEAD 01e904a on 2026-10-25 in [`MeetingSourceScanner.swift`](../../../daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/MeetingSourceScanner.swift):

1. **DET5 (2026-07-12, one day after PERF6) admits a lone native unconditionally.** `shouldWalkControlAX` now ends `return titleMatch || processAudioIsRunningInput || isFrontmost || isLoneNative`, and `enumerateCandidates` computes `let loneNative = apps.reduce(into: 0) { ... } == 1` over `nativeBundles`. Its doc comment justifies the admission as "it is the only native meeting app running, so the walk is cheap". The owner's usual setup is Teams always open and no other native meeting app, so `isLoneNative` is true on every scan of the day, and the walk it calls cheap is the one PERF6 measured as the idle drain.
2. **The scanner's Teams recognizer admits almost any titled window, and it predates PERF6.** The Teams branch of `MeetingSourceScanner.isActiveMeetingWindow` (in place since 2026-05-21) rejects only exact chrome words (`"chat"`, `"calendar"`, `"settings"`, ...) and otherwise returns `!lowered.isEmpty` for a bare title, or `true` for any `<lead> | Microsoft Teams` whose lead is not a chrome word. Its comment states the contract: it "prefers false-positives ... over false-negatives". Since Teams dropped the ` | Microsoft Teams` suffix on 2026-10-08 ([END14](./END14.md)), a chat window reads `Chat | Max Doleh`, which is neither empty nor an exact chrome word, so `titleMatch` is true for an idle Teams and the gate opens even with `isLoneNative` removed. `MeetingSourceScorer.hasCorroboratingSignal`'s doc says the same from the scoring side: "Teams/Slack title recognizers match chat threads and calendar windows".
3. **The log shows the walk running on an idle Teams.** [DET12](./DET12.md)'s read of 2026-08-19 lists 13 Teams `candidate_dropped` rows between 00:45 and 14:23 with `title_match: true` and every control signal false, and [DET13](./DET13.md) counted 293 of 294 Teams `candidate_dropped` rows as title-only. Those rows are throttled by signature, so they undercount scans; each is a scan whose gate was open and whose walk found nothing.

**What one such scan costs.** With the gate open, `collectSignals` runs `MeetingAXHandleBuilder.findCallingControlsToolbar`, `findAllLeaveButtons` and `findAllMuteButtons` ([`MeetingAXHandleBuilder.swift`](../../../daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/MeetingAXHandleBuilder.swift)) as three separate passes, each calling `listWindows(axApp:)` and running its own `findButton` or `findToolbar` DFS per window with a fresh budget (`private static let maxNodes = 4000`). With no call up nothing matches, so each pass walks each window to the end of its tree or to 4000 nodes. Every node costs two synchronous cross-process reads (`kAXRoleAttribute`, `kAXChildrenAttribute`), plus three more (`textBlob`: title, help, description) for every `AXToolbar` or `AXGroup` in the toolbar pass and every button or checkbox in the Leave and Mute passes. The cadence is every 12 s idle and every 3 s for one poll after any launch, activation, deactivation or termination of a browser or meeting app (`MeetingDiscoveryWatcher.wireWorkspaceObservers`, `self.cadence.noteActivity()`), on `scanQueue` at `.userInitiated`. Every request is serviced on Teams' own main thread, so the cost lands on both processes. The node count of an idle Teams tree has never been measured: `maxNodes`' doc comment says a meeting window is "well under 100 nodes", which says nothing about a chat window full of messages.

**The browser leg (DETECTION-5).** A plain browser admitted on a strong title is built with `preTitleMatch: true`, so `shouldWalkControlAX` is true on every scan of a Meet call. `findAllMuteButtons` short-circuits (`guard let app = appNameByBundle[bundleID] else { return [] }`, and no browser is in that map), but `findAllLeaveButtons` does not: it walks every browser window and calls `matchesLeave`, whose `switch bundleID` ends `default: return false`. So every browser window is walked to the budget, reading `textBlob` on every button, for a result that is false by construction.

The live idle numbers taken on 2026-10-25 do not bear on this: Teams was not running on the review Mac, so no native candidate existed to walk.

## Scope

The seam is `MeetingSourceScanner.collectSignals` and `MeetingSourceScanner.shouldWalkControlAX`, plus the walk functions in `MeetingAXHandleBuilder`. Items 2 and 3 change no detection outcome and land whatever the owner decides on item 1.

1. **Gate the walk on live-call evidence for natives that have it (owner decision first, see the walkthrough).** DET12 restored the one admission that is evidence of a call rather than a guess at one: `processAudioIsRunningInput`, read for exactly the natives `MeetingSourceScanner.readsProcessAudio(bundleID:kind:)` answers true for (Teams and Zoom at HEAD 01e904a). For those bundles, admit the walk on process audio or on a strict call-window title, and stop admitting on the permissive `titleMatch` and on `isLoneNative`. Zoom's scanner recognizer is already strict (`lowered.contains("zoom meeting")`, launcher rejected). For Teams, the gate's title test is a localized meeting stem from `MeetingTitlePatterns`' `teamsTokens` (`"meeting"`, `"besprechung"`, ...) without the `"microsoft teams"` suffix token, which would admit every window again if Teams restored its suffix; the compact call panel (`Meeting compact view | ...`) passes, `Chat | Max Doleh` does not. Natives with no audio read (Slack) keep DET5's `isLoneNative` and `isFrontmost` admissions, since nothing else protects the renamed-window case DET5 was filed for there; Webex keeps always walking. Whether `isFrontmost` survives for Teams and Zoom is the owner's call (walkthrough step 1). `signals.titleMatch` keeps its value and weight for scoring and for `candidate_dropped`; only the walk admission changes, so the scorer and DET12's evidence rows read exactly as before.
2. **One DFS per window for all three predicates.** Replace the three passes with one walk per window that tests the toolbar needles on toolbar and group nodes and the Leave and Mute matchers on button and checkbox nodes, reads `textBlob` once per node that needs it, keeps the per-window `maxNodes` budget and `maxDepth`, and stops when every predicate it still needs has hit or the budget is spent. Within one budget it visits the same nodes in the same order as each separate pass, so it finds exactly the same first hits, and it lists the windows once instead of three times. To make that checkable, make the DFS generic over the node and an attribute reader (the live AX calls by default), so a fake tree can drive it and count reads.
3. **No Leave walk for browsers.** Skip the Leave predicate for `kind == .browser` (plain tabs and PWAs), since `matchesLeave` returns false for every non-native bundle. Keep the toolbar predicate: a name-only PWA admission has no other AX corroborator, and the toolbar's weight decides contested scans, so narrowing it is a scoring change with its own evidence owed.

## Explicitly not

- Lowering `maxNodes` for discovery. The "well under 100 nodes" claim is unmeasured against current Teams, and a budget that cuts off a real call's controls drops a real meeting, the worst failure class. Measure a node count first if it is ever wanted.
- Batching reads through `AXUIElementCopyMultipleAttributeValues`, or dropping `scanQueue` from `.userInitiated` to `.utility`. Worth doing only if the post-change sample (Acceptance) still shows walk cost; batching also changes per-attribute error handling.
- Tightening `isActiveMeetingWindow`'s permissive Teams branch itself. It feeds `titleMatch`, which scores and logs; that is a detection change, and Teams title matching belongs to [END14](./END14.md) and [DET15](./DET15.md). This task only stops it from buying a walk.
- A per-window "no call controls here" cache across scans. Item 1 removes the idle walk outright; a cache is the fallback if the owner keeps `isFrontmost` and the sample shows frontmost walks still matter.
- Scanning while a prompt or recording is up, and scans queueing behind a slow one: the discovery-scheduling task filed from the same review (DETECTION-4 ([PERF17](./PERF17.md)), DETECTION-8 ([PERF17](./PERF17.md))). The in-call mute backstop's 1 Hz walk: its own task from the same review (DETECTION-6 ([PERF18](./PERF18.md))). The per-recording `MeetingAXHandleBuilder.build` walks on main: [PERF10](./PERF10.md) item 3.

## Acceptance

- `MeetingSourceScannerTests`, the gate (item 1, per the owner's decision): a lone, backgrounded Teams with a `Chat | Max Doleh` window and process audio false does not walk; the same Teams with process audio true walks; a Teams window titled `Meeting compact view | Weekly sync` walks; a lone Slack with a renamed window still walks; Webex still always walks; the `isFrontmost` case is pinned whichever way the owner decides. DET5's `test_lone_native_walks_even_with_a_renamed_window` and `test_frontmost_native_walks_even_without_a_title_match` are rewritten to the decided behaviour with the decision cited, not deleted.
- Scoring is untouched: a test that the chat-titled idle Teams still carries `titleMatch == true` in its signals.
- The combined walk (item 2), over a fake tree: for fixture trees with the toolbar, Leave and Mute hits in the first window, in a later window, absent, and beyond the budget, one combined walk returns the same three results as the three separate walks, lists the windows once, and reads each node's role and children at most once.
- The browser leg (item 3): a browser candidate's signals are built with zero Leave-predicate reads (counting reader), and its toolbar predicate still runs.
- `cd daemon && swift build` and `swift test` green, new test count in the commit body; `bash scripts/ci-local.sh` green. No new event is required.
- Evidence after install (confirms, does not gate): a 60 s `sample MeetingPipe` with Teams open, idle and backgrounded shows no `findButton` or `findToolbar` frames under the `com.meetingpipe.MeetingDiscoveryWatcher.scan` queue, and the owner's next Teams call still produces a Teams `discovery_shadow_pick` (`mp logs --action discovery_shadow_pick`).
