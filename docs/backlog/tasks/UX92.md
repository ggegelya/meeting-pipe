# UX92: Notifications are posted fire-and-forget, a denied Notifications permission appears in no menu row or Finish setup item, and the only warning before the silence backstop stops a live recording is a notification

Band origin: filed 2026-10-25 from the 2026-10-25 observability audit band ([observability audit](../../audits/2026-10-25-observability-audit.md) row O12, UI-3, LG-11 and SW-15). Status and priority live in this task's ToC row in [meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md](../meetingpipe-q6-backlog.md).

## Context

Re-verified against `main` at `01e904a` on 2026-10-25 by code reading. The Notifications authorization on the audit Mac was not probed (it needs the app's own identity), and its `events.jsonl` (2026-10-12 to 2026-10-24) has no `silence_notified` or `auto_stop_silence` row, so the backstop path has not fired there.

**No post is checked or recorded.** `Notifier.swift` calls `UNUserNotificationCenter.current().add(...)` 12 times (in `notifyDone`, `notifyFailure`, `notifyStillMeeting`, `post(title:body:)`, the skip, mic and capture notices), none with a completion handler. The authorization result is `Log.main.info("Notification auth granted: \(granted)")` in `Notifier.requestAuthorization`, os_log only. On 2026-10-16 the owner's log has four `coordinator/pipeline_failed` rows (07:33:59Z, 07:38:28Z, 07:38:59Z, 09:46:34Z); each implies a failure banner, and no row says one was posted or delivered.

**A denied permission is invisible outside Preferences.** `StatusBarModel.hasPendingPermissionIssue` and `permissionConsequences` cover the microphone, Screen Recording and Accessibility only, and `SetupChecklist` reuses `hasPendingPermissionIssue` for its `.permissions` item. `PermissionsCenter.refreshNotifications` reads `notificationSettings().authorizationStatus` for the Preferences row only, and its `Kind.notifications` description ("Used for processing-done, mic-only-fallback, and still-meeting prompts") undersells it: every failure notice, the disk-full force-stop, the stop-stall notice and the recording-start failure also depend on it. `authorizationStatus` alone also misses an app that is authorized with banners turned off (`alertSetting == .disabled`). `mp doctor`'s Swift probes in `DoctorCommand` include `permission.microphone` and `permission.screen_recording` and nothing for notifications.

**The one warning before an irreversible stop is notification-only.** `MeetingSessionController.handleIdleNotify` logs `silence_notified` and calls `notifyStillMeeting()` ("Still in a meeting?"); `handleIdleAutoStop` then stops the recording on schedule. The HUD (`RecordingHUDWindow`) has states for system audio degraded, mic lost, off the record and confirming end, and none for this. Under Focus, with notifications denied, or while presenting, the owner gets no warning before the backstop ends a live recording. The `silence_notified` event records that the app meant to warn, not that a banner was shown.

**Narrowed from the finder.** The finder also listed the wrong-microphone warning (`notifyInputDeviceMismatch`) and "Could not start recording" as notification-only. The first is advisory and posted before capture starts; the second already writes `recording_error`, and its copy is UX93's. Neither is in this task's scope beyond item 1's recording of the post.

## Scope

1. **One post path that records.** Route every `add(...)` in `Notifier` through one private helper that takes a `kind` (a small enum: `done`, `failure`, `still_meeting`, `skip_late`, `mic`, `capture`, `accessibility`, `info`) and an optional stem, passes a completion handler, and emits `daemon/notification_posted` with `kind`, `stem` when known, and `error` (domain and code) when `add` failed. A failed add also writes one `Log.writeLine("daemon", ...)` line. Never the title or body text (a body can carry a meeting name).
2. **Record the authorization.** At launch, after `requestAuthorization` returns, and on each change seen by `refreshNotifications`, emit `daemon/notification_auth` with `status` (`granted`, `denied`, `not_determined`, `provisional`) and `alerts` (`enabled`, `disabled`). Read `alertSetting` beside `authorizationStatus` and treat an authorized app with alerts disabled as off for items 3 and 4.
3. **Notifications in the menu and Finish setup.** `StatusBarModel.Inputs` gains the notifications state; `permissionConsequences` adds "⚠ Notifications off: failures and the still-meeting warning will not reach you" when denied or alerts disabled. `hasPendingPermissionIssue` does not change (notifications stay optional, so the generic permission row does not fire), but `SetupChecklist` gains a `notifications` item linking to Preferences ▸ Permissions. Update `PermissionsCenter.Kind.notifications`'s description to say failures depend on it.
4. **A doctor probe.** `permission.notifications` in `DoctorCommand` (WARN when denied or alerts disabled). Check at pickup that `UNUserNotificationCenter` answers from the `MeetingPipe doctor` process inside the bundle; if it does not, the in-app doctor sheet passes the app's own reading in, and the CLI reports `unknown` rather than guessing.
5. **A HUD state for the auto-stop nudge.** When `handleIdleNotify` fires, the HUD shows "No audio, stopping soon" on the recording label (the `setConfirmingEnd` pattern: a label and color change, no new card) until `keepRecordingFromNudge`, a sound, or the stop. Off the record and mic lost keep precedence over it, as they do over confirming end.
6. **Docs in the same commit.** [GUIDE.md](../../GUIDE.md) section 10, "The recording did not stop when the call did": the recording panel says it is about to stop, not only the notification. Section 10, "Nothing recorded at all" says "check all four" permissions; add a sentence that Notifications is the one that only costs you warnings, and that the menu now says when it is off. Section 10, "Start here": Finish setup lists Notifications when they are off.

## Explicitly not / out of scope

- Setting `interruptionLevel` (time-sensitive) on failure notices. It needs an entitlement decision and changes how Focus treats the app; the owner decides that separately.
- Retrying a failed `add`. A failed post is rare and the event plus the menu row are the fallback.
- A general `permission_changed` event for all four kinds: OBS5. This task records notifications only, because no other code reads its state.
- Recording whether a delivered banner was seen. macOS does not say, and the click handlers already log the actions they route.
- Failure-notice titles and the lost Details after relaunch: UX93. Jargon in notification copy: UX86.
- Moving the Tutorial's HUD step: the tour rings the HUD itself, not its label; if a `TutorialAnchor` names this label, update the tour in the same commit per `daemon/CLAUDE.md`.
