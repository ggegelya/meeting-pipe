# Field evidence from daily use

Companion to the independent audit of 2026-11-12 (`2026-11-12-independent-audit.md`, in this folder), whose executive summary carries the conclusions drawn from this file.

Added 2026-11-13. The owner supplied a pack from the work laptop where the app is used every day: the daemon and pipeline logs (114,553 daemon events, 2026-09-29 to 2026-11-13, 38 active days) and the files of 113 meetings from 2026-09-14 to 2026-11-13, without audio. Five analysts mined it, each reading source to explain what a log line means, and a sixth recomputed the headline numbers independently. That laptop was rebuilt on 2026-10-12 and 2026-11-12, so the logs cover three builds: era 1 (before 2026-10-12), era 2 (2026-10-12 to 2026-11-12) and era 3 (from 2026-11-12, close to HEAD).

Nothing from a meeting is quoted here: no titles, names, transcript or summary text. Meetings are named by stem (their start timestamp) and events by action name.

## How far to trust the numbers

The checker recomputed 60 of the 166 reported metrics with its own scripts: 47 agreed, 13 were close, 0 disagreed, 0 could not be recomputed.

All 60 metrics were recomputed from the pack with new scripts (python3 -I, kept in the session scratchpad none of the analysts' scripts was opened). 47 agree outright, 13 are close (items 10, 18, 21, 22, 23, 25, 31, 33, 43, 44, 48, 56, 59), none is wrong and none was impossible to recompute. Rule used: yes means every sub-number reproduces under the definition as stated; close means it reproduces only under a definition the claim does not state, or one sub-number is off. The close ones: item 10 (99 episodes needs no-source recordings chained across apps, strict same-app gives 102; storm median is 10.5, not 10); item 18 (count, membership and denominators reproduce, the confirmed and unresolved split is a judgement rule); item 21 (median 866 s needs the proxy row to fall after the prompt closed, else 704 s); item 22 (all counts exact, but the last teardown row sits 0.84 s inside a launch sequence, not before the launch); item 23 (21 final held rows, not 24; min and median match); item 25 (shadow-pick latency needs a 30 s cap); item 31 (5,947 and 7 on strict windows); item 33 (the per-hour rate counts each first verdict as a change; strict median 122, not 128); item 43 (14, not 13, on AX probe rows alone; 219 and 228 are row counts); item 44 (exact on the poller reading alone, 16 intervals with AX probe rows added); item 48 (75.4 s is a median of medians, pooled 72.6 s); item 56 (era 3 is 0 of 6, not 1 of 7); item 59 (shortfalls are net of an unstated 0.11 s baseline). Percentile conventions are mixed: items 6, 14, 23 and 25 match linear interpolation only, items 33, 39 and 41 match nearest rank only, and items 23 and 41 report the same 38 lags both ways. Window conventions are mixed too: items 15, 22 and 23 end a recording at the stop decision, items 26, 36, 47 and 51 at recording_stopped, and items 32 and 34 need item 31's window that opens up to 5 s early. What the recount adds: 306 of the 318 dismissed prompts were shown after their engagement's ended row was already logged; 605 of the 1,747 era-1 in-recording re-resolves belong to the stopping phase of one 6.6 s recording; teardown-source rows stop at an ordinary relaunch on 2026-10-30 with no rebuild (log-source rows are last seen on 2026-10-23, the last recording day before it), and 7 of 8 log lines after it are still stale; 28 lifecycle_end_held rows are logged after the stop decision; the resumed recording 20261112-174606 ended its lifecycle 16 ms after starting and ran 648 s with no end detection. Audit matches by ID: F046 (the resume itself, item 4), F036 and G027 (suppressed or overridden AX readings, items 44 and 46), F031, G024 and G025 (pair dwell, remembered window-gone, veto freshness, items 13, 16, 24), G001 (held-tail trim point, item 19), F004 (source-less recordings with no gate, item 29). Field patterns with no finding in the audit digest: the 2026-10-08 to 10-12 prompt storm (items 7 to 10); 16,861 call_teardown_source_missing rows (item 22); 75 of 82 blind clears of a muted latch followed by a muted reading (items 38 and 39); SlimCore lines delivered a median 42 s late (items 41 to 43); mute-listener bursts whose size tracks the count of earlier default-input subscribes, consistent with listeners firing after their unsubscribed row, and the 15,020-row burst on 2026-10-12 (items 51 to 56); the resumed recording with no end detection (item 19). Limits: owner speech rests on one inferred speaker label and on segment times offset from recording_started; the pack has no audio, so false-end and missed-end labels are inferences from signals, not ground truth.

## Sessions, prompts and end decisions

The daemon log (2026-09-29 to 2026-11-13, 38 active days, 25 with recordings) holds 417 prompts and 74 recordings: 66 Teams, 3 Zoom, 1 Chrome and 4 hand-started with no source, 31.4 recorded hours, median 18 minutes, about 3 per recording day. The newest build (era 3, from 2026-11-12, 6 recordings) is the worst for ends: none of the 6 ended both promptly and by itself. Four Teams ends waited the full 60 s pair dwell because Teams' teardown line never confirmed one, a resumed recording ran 380 s past its call, and the one Zoom recording had its end hold reverted and was stopped by hand. The resumed recording is a new defect: at a mid-call engagement the initial window-gone reading landed 2 ms before the initial process-audio reading, the engine went from starting to ended, the end was dropped because an idle-origin start was in flight (F045, reached through F046), and the recording then had no end detection at all. Era 1 (before the 2026-10-12 rebuild) holds the prompt storm: from 2026-10-08T14:31Z a Teams title change made the window signal read gone, 315 Teams prompts were dismissed by a lifecycle end (307 of all 318 dismissed prompts were on screen under 0.5 s, 219 prompts fell inside one 33 minute call) and 4 recordings were chopped at 7 to 86 s. That is the whole of 381 ended against 106 in_meeting: 318 ended rows come straight from starting with no recording behind them, 62 confirm a held end and 1 is an instant pair, while in_meeting is 69 recorder-armed confirmations plus 37 reverted holds. END14 in era 2 is holding (136 vetoed window-gone ends, no lone window-led end since, 1.08 and then 1.00 prompts per prompted episode), but the title mismatch itself was never repaired: the window signal read gone for 54.9% of era-2 and 99.7% of era-3 Teams recording time, so the era-3 pair rule is in practice process audio alone for 60 s (the standing precondition of F031 and G024, with no wrong end observed yet). Recordings ended by a lifecycle verdict 63 times, by hand 10 times and by the silence backstop once; in eras 1 and 2 a mic release was confirmed by the Leave button or the window in a median 0.5 to 0.7 s. Of 16 ends followed by a same-app call within 15 minutes, 6 were false ends (the 4 storm chops, a mic-switch end on 2026-10-22 that the owner repaired by hand after 23 s, and a source-less recording of a browser meeting stopped by the Chrome adapter on 2026-11-11), 1 was a leave and rejoin that era 3 resumed, 5 were new calls and 4 cannot be resolved. Missed or late ends: 562 s and 63 s in era 1 where a healthy Leave reading blocked a mic-release end, 315 s on a source-less recording in era 2, and in era 3 the 380 s case plus 60 s on every Teams end. The larger loss is at the start and is not a detection miss: of 99 prompts that stayed up for the owner 61 were accepted (median 3.9 s), 32 timed out at 30 s with default skip and 6 were skipped; 9 of those 38 were then started by hand 27 s to 27 minutes late and 29 were never recorded, with the call visible a median 14.4 minutes afterwards (whether any miss was deliberate cannot be known). Smaller start defects: a hand start within 10 s of a prompt vanishing records with no source and so no end detection (3 of the 4 source-less recordings), a call that began 8 s after a skipped one went 25.6 minutes with no prompt (consistent with the documented skip-latch blind spot), all 3 Zoom prompts trailed a visible mute button by 12 to 64 s, and 1 of 7 Chrome prompts became a recording. call_teardown_source_missing is one failed listing of Teams' log directory by the 1 Hz teardown watcher: 16,861 rows (14.7% of the log), about one per minute around the clock until 2026-10-30 and none since, for a reason the pack cannot show. It never made the oracle unavailable and never ended a recording early; its cost is a tail that restarts at end of file after each failure, and it is dwarfed by the oracle confirming 0 of 59 Teams ends. Observability is thin exactly here: manual stops, the reason a prompt left the screen and mic-busy spans are not in the log, and 13 rotation-truncated lines include 2 lifecycle ended rows.

**Findings.**

- **Era 3: a resumed recording ran with its lifecycle engine already ended, so nothing could end it** (high; new defect; audit F045 (demonstrated once: an end that arrives while an idle-origin start is in flight is dropped), F046 (the resume path was the trigger). The initial-reading race is not in the digest.). The only recording_resumed_after_end (2026-11-12T15:46:06.606Z, resumed_from 20261112-172307, since_end_sec 38.6). Order at that engagement: ax_leave_button_state initial healthy at .597, lifecycle/starting .598, shareable_content_window_present initial false .614, lifecycle/ended (leading shareable_content_window_gone, confirmed_by empty) .614, process_audio_is_running_input initial true .616, state idle to recording .677. Stem 20261112-174606 has 0 lifecycle/in_meeting rows; process audio went false at 15:50:34.737Z and the Leave button invalid at 15:50:34.823Z with no ending_provisional; the owner stopped it at 15:56:54.489Z, 380 s later (tail after last speech 382 s). Across the 5 era-3 Teams engagements with both initial readings, the mic reading led the window reading by 4, 4, 7 and 11 ms and trailed it by 2 ms once; the window read false at 2 of the 5 and 1 of those 2 was lost. Era 2 had the mic first in 58 of 58. Build eras: Era 3 only, n=1, on the first day of the newest build. Still present at HEAD by reading. Code: `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle/Adapters/NativeLifecycleAdapter.swift:165 (signals start on independent queues); daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle/PromotionEngine.swift:365 (the veto sees only readings already recorded); daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/MeetingSessionController.swift:1274 (the end is dropped when the state is idle)`. Recommendation: In starting, do not let a weak end lead until process audio has reported once for this engagement or a short bound has passed. Latch an end seen during any in-flight start, not only a prompt-origin one. When confirmRecording finds the engine in ended, re-engage. Log a row when a recording is running with the engine in ended, and fence it.
- **A recording with no source is stopped by any engaged adapter's starting to ended, with no hold** (high; new defect; audit None in the digest. F004 is about source-less recordings too, with a different symptom, and was not exercised.). Stem 20261111-155951 (bundle_id manual, era 2). discovery_shadow_pick chose Chrome at score 2 (title only) at 13:59:31.063Z and engaged it with no starting; the owner started by hand at 13:59:52.402Z; lifecycle/starting for Chrome came 767.8 s after the pick, during the recording; lifecycle/ended (leading browser_tab_title_left_meet_pattern, confirmed_by empty, predecessor starting) at 14:14:28.163Z stopped the recording 6 ms later at 876 s. The window matched again and lifecycle/starting fired 8.2 s after the stop; that prompt timed out at 14:15:06.823Z and nothing more was recorded. The end came from starting, where neither the 120 s tab-title floor of era 2 nor the END17 hold applies. Build eras: Observed once, in era 2. Present in the newest code by reading only: era 3 has no source-less recording. Code: `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipe/MeetingSessionController.swift:1230 (an end is matched to the recording by bundle only when the recording has a source) and :251 (a starting during a recording is confirmed only for a matching source); daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle/PromotionEngine.swift:371`. Recommendation: Ignore ends from an adapter that never reached in_meeting for this recording, or attach the engaged adapter's source to a hand start and run it through the normal hold. Do not engage on a score-2 title-only pick.
- **Teams' teardown line confirmed no end in six weeks; in era 3 every Teams end waits the 60 s dwell** (medium; new defect; audit None in the digest. G001 and G026 concern the trim that this tail feeds; all 4 field trims were mode suggested, so neither was exercised.). 0 of 59 Teams lifecycle ends were confirmed by the oracle. The only 2 lifecycle_provisional_end_confirmed rows (2026-11-09T16:02:09.318Z and 2026-11-12T07:39:32.107Z, teardown_observed, held_sec 0) were logged 0.22 s and 0.34 s after lifecycle/ended and after the stop decision. Era 3: 4 of 4 mic-led Teams ends took 60.01 to 60.07 s from ending_provisional to ended with oracle_available true, and recorder/held_end_trimmed marked 60.1 to 60.6 s each (242 s in total, mode suggested, audio kept). log_written_sec_ago when the 24 final holds opened: min 129 s, median 878 s, none under 15 s. Code: `daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle/CallTeardownWatcher.swift:247 (the row) and :233 (a failed listing clears the tail position); daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/MicGate/SlimCoreMuteLog.swift:239 (the next resolve starts at end of file, so bytes written across a failure are never read); daemon/Sources/MeetingPipeCore/Lifecycle/CallTeardownOracle.swift:136`. ## Mic, mute and capture health

All 74 logged recordings (31.59 h; 66 Teams, 3 Zoom, 1 Chrome, 4 manual; 24, 44 and 6 by build era) produced a merged two-channel file, and the mic gate never altered audio: every window disagreement ran under the capture-first policy (blind on 43 of 43), the NDA flag is false on 113 of 113 sidecars, and the only redaction in six weeks is one owner-requested off-the-record span of 955 s in 20261013-170136. The worst defect is in the mute fusion. Teams SlimCore log lines reach the adapter late (38 events, median 41.9 s after their own timestamp, p90 1,338 s, max 2,607 s, 26 older than 5 s), the gate stamps each as fresh and lets it outrank the live button for 5 s, and the contradicting button reading is suppressed and never sent again. That produced 17 gate-versus-button disagreements longer than 5 s (5,139 s in 15 recordings); in the 8 where the gate said muted while the button read unmuted the owner is transcribed speaking for 1,294 s, 896 s of it in 20261012-182939, where a line written before the recording began arrived 56 s into it and held muted_by_app for 1,999 s. In the capture-first mode in use this cost one false still-in-a-meeting nudge; under the regulated gate that speech would have been zero-filled. The log oracle earned little in return: for 32 of its 38 lines a button reading of the same state was already in the event log before the line arrived, and 13 lines arrived when the latest button reading already showed the opposite state. Blind clears fired 108 times, and of the 82 that dropped a latched mute the next confident reading was muted again in 75. Capture losses are small and sit on 2026-10-15: one SCStream start hang cost the first 33.2 s of far-side audio in 20261015-170719, the same recording also lost 10.7 s of mic and ended with a system channel 15.9 s shorter than its mic, and 20261015-144207 ended with a system channel 14.4 s shorter than its mic, none of it reported by any event. In 5 recordings of the two older builds the recorder's own stop began 5 to 24 s after the app had decided to stop, with the mic still capturing. ## Daemon health and cross-check of the code audit

The daemon itself was stable in the 45.9 days the event log still covers (2026-09-29T09:45Z to 2026-11-13T07:44Z): 12 launches, no crash signature on stderr, no launch that interrupted a recording or a pipeline run, and all 74 recordings produced a final file with both mic and system audio. The worst problem in my area is that the event log is mostly noise and the noise decides what history survives: three families (HAL subscribe/subscribed/unsubscribed, call_teardown_source_missing, process_audio_object resolved/unresolved) are 79.5 percent of the bytes, 56 single minutes hold 54.7 percent of all rows, and rotation therefore kept only 45.9 days and lost everything before 2026-09-29, including the events for 40 of the 113 meetings in the pack. The build that ran until the 2026-10-12 rebuild had a prompt flash loop that is audit finding F030 in the field: 307 lifecycle ends fired within 1 s of a lifecycle start from a first reading, and 322 of 357 prompts were dismissed without a choice, 285 of them in under 0.1 s, with 222 prompts on 2026-10-08 alone. The 2026-10-12 rebuild removed that loop (0 first-reading ends, 55 prompts for 44 recordings) and cut HAL churn from 3,391 to 1,333 rows per recorded hour; the 2026-11-12 rebuild cut it to 275 but added a new chatter source (ax_mute_button_state_kept, 39 percent of era C rows), a fixed 60 s wait before every detected end, and the held-end trim and resume-after-end paths. In its first 22 hours the newest build already showed F046 once (a new Teams call recorded 38.6 s after the previous one ended, with no prompt) and ran the G001 trim path on 4 of 6 recordings, always in suggested mode with 60 s tails; the destructive form of G001 was not seen because no hold in 83 lasted 300 s and no meeting used an NDA workflow. Permissions were never lost in the window: the 48 permission_granted rows are 12 launches times 4 kinds, logged at every start, and both rebuilds had all four within 2 s. There is no start, stop or heartbeat event, so uptime cannot be measured; hours with logged activity fell from 20.3 per day to 1.2 per day only because one noise source stopped on 2026-10-24. The live logs contain test-suite output: 67,966 of pipeline.log's 79,882 lines are a Swift test fixture, and daemon.log for May and June carries 1,615 pipeline-queued lines against 112 recordings.
